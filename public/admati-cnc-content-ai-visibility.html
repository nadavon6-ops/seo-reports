English · Precision CNC page
Admati Agencies' advantage as a CNC supplier goes beyond the machining itself. The company provides production engineering and outsourcing services to businesses moving their manufacturing to South-East Asia. It also handles development, factory audits and supply chain management until the parts reach the customer. Admati's QA department checks dimensions with coordinate measuring machines (CMMs), height gauges, calipers and micrometers. Customers get everything under one roof, from design through production. Admati serves companies in the medical, military, industrial and semiconductor industries.
