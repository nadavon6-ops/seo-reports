What Happens Next — 3 Workstreams
Now that we know what people are searching for and how your competitors rank, we use that data to take action. Every step below is based directly on the research we already completed — nothing is chosen randomly.
1

Optimization Reports for Existing Pages

Your website already has pages that appear in Google search results — but many of them are not fully optimized. This means small, targeted improvements to these pages can push them higher in the rankings and bring in more traffic, without creating anything new. We analyze each page against the keywords we researched and provide a specific report on what to change.
  • We review your main pages, product pages, and blog articles one by one
  • For each page, we check: is the title aligned with what people search? Is the content complete? Are the right pages linked together?
  • You receive a clear report per page with exactly what to update
2

New Blog Topic Proposals

During the keyword research and competitive analysis, we found topics that your potential customers are searching for — but your website doesn't have content about yet. Your competitors may already cover some of these topics. New articles fill these gaps, giving Google more reasons to show your site in search results. Every topic we propose is based on actual search data — not guesswork.
  • Each topic comes from the keyword research — it targets real search queries with proven demand
  • We cross-check against competitor content to find topics where you can add unique value
  • Every topic is submitted to you for approval before any writing begins
  • After approval, we prepare a detailed brief so the writer knows exactly what to cover
3

Support Articles for External Link Building

Google doesn't just look at your website — it also looks at how many other reputable websites link to you. The more quality sites that reference your content, the more Google trusts your site and ranks it higher. We create articles designed to be published on other websites, with links pointing back to your key pages. This builds your site's credibility over time.
  • Articles are written around your most important keywords and topics
  • Published on relevant external websites to create inbound links
  • Each link points to a strategic page on your site, strengthening its ranking potential

Quarterly Roadmap
Each phase builds on the one before it. We start with choosing the right keywords, then optimize what you already have, then create new content to fill gaps, and finally build external credibility. Here is the sequence:
1
Phase 1

Keyword Finalization & Prioritization

We select and prioritize the final list of keywords from the 573 we researched. Not all keywords are equal — we focus first on the ones with the best combination of search volume, business relevance, and realistic ranking potential.

Why this matters: Everything that follows — which pages we optimize, which articles we write, which topics we target — is driven by this keyword list. Getting this right is the foundation of the entire strategy.
In Progress
2
Phase 2

Existing Page Optimization Reports

Once the keywords are confirmed, we audit each existing page on your site and produce a report showing exactly what needs to change — titles, descriptions, content, internal links — so that each page is aligned with the keywords it should rank for.

Why this matters: Your site already has pages that Google indexes. Optimizing them is the fastest way to improve rankings because you're improving something Google already knows about, rather than starting from scratch.
Up Next
3
Phase 3

New Blog Topic Planning & Approval

We propose new article topics based on the keyword gaps and competitor analysis we already completed. Each topic targets a specific group of search queries that your site doesn't currently have content for. All topics are submitted for your approval before writing begins.

Why this matters: If people search for something related to your services and your site has no page about it, you won't appear in the results. New articles close these gaps and give Google more entry points to discover your site.
Up Next
4
Phase 4

Support Content for Authority & Link Building

We create quality articles to be published on other websites, with links pointing back to your most important pages. These are not random articles — they are built around your top keywords and designed to signal to Google that your site is a trusted source in your industry.

Why this matters: Google uses links from other websites as a signal of trust. The more reputable sites that link to you, the more authority your site gains, and the higher it ranks for competitive search terms.
