Current Strengths

What is already performing well

The following are established assets. The recommendations in this report build upon them.

Sound technical foundation

HTTPS, mobile-friendly rendering, an open robots.txt, a complete XML sitemap index, and modern WebP images with alt text. Yoast SEO is installed and generating structured data.

Brand and local dominance

Position 1 for "makoa", "villas by the cove" and "wailea inn", and Local Pack position 1 for several "luxury villa Maui" searches, supported by strong Google Business Profile signals.

AI search presence

The site is already cited in Google AI Overviews for brand and "luxury villa in Hawaii" queries, ahead of most competitors of a similar age.

Effective homepage

A well-constructed title tag, a clear meta description, a single H1, and complete Open Graph and Twitter Card markup for accurate social sharing.

Logical site architecture

Dedicated, indexable pages for accommodations, weddings, events, wellness, amenities, activities, location, concierge and FAQ. These are the appropriate pages for the business.

Aligned content strategy

For a single-property luxury resort, conversion-focused property and location pages are the priority. The growth path is optimizing these pages, with no requirement for a blog.

Recommendations

Prioritized optimization plan

Each recommendation is a specific action to be carried out on the live website and is tied to a real page. Every action notes the level of effort and the responsible party (content team, developer or hosting provider) so the work can be assigned and completed. Items are ordered by impact relative to effort.

01

Add meta descriptions to every inner page

High impact
Finding

Only the homepage has a meta description. The property, weddings, amenities, activities, location and FAQ pages have none, so Google generates the snippet automatically.

Business impact

The search snippet functions as free advertising in the results. A purpose-written, benefit-led description increases click-through on the rankings already held.

Recommended action Low effort · content team

In Yoast, write a unique 140 to 160 character meta description for each page and each property, leading with the offer and location. For example: "Private two-bedroom oceanfront villa in Kihei, Maui. Heated pool, daily housekeeping, steps from the beach. Book direct with Makoa Resorts."

02

Resolve duplicate property URLs

High impact
Finding

Each property exists at two live, indexable URLs: a marketing page (for example /villas-by-the-cove/) and a listing page (/accommodation/villas-by-the-cove-estate/). Both return a 200 status.

Business impact

Two pages addressing the same topic divide ranking signals and compete with one another. This is evident in the data, where the homepage and the property page both compete for "villas by the cove" rather than a single page ranking decisively.

Recommended action Medium effort · developer

Select one canonical URL per property (the /accommodation/ listing pages currently perform best), apply a 301 redirect from the duplicate to it, and update all internal links. This consolidates authority onto a single strong page for each property.

03

Implement lodging and per-property structured data

High impact
Finding

The website outputs only a generic Organization schema containing a logo. There is no address, telephone, geo-coordinate or social profile data, and no lodging, property, offer or review markup on any page.

Business impact

Resort, per-property and Review schema enable Google and AI engines to interpret pricing, amenities, ratings and location. This is the mechanism that produces star ratings, "from $X per night" labels and AI-answer citations.

Recommended action Medium effort · developer

(1) Upgrade the homepage to Resort / LodgingBusiness schema with the full address (2121 Ili'ili Road, Kihei, HI 96753), telephone, geo-coordinates, price range and sameAs links to the Google Business Profile and social pages. (2) Add Accommodation / Product and Offer markup to each property, including bedrooms, occupancy, amenities and price. (3) Add AggregateRating / Review markup using the guest testimonials already published on the site.

04

Add FAQ structured data to the FAQ page

Medium impact
Finding

The website has a complete FAQ page, but it carries no FAQPage structured data.

Business impact

FAQ markup increases the likelihood of questions surfacing in AI Overviews and the "People Also Ask" panel, providing high visibility for booking questions such as cancellation, check-in, pets and minimum stay.

Recommended action Low effort · content team

Mark up each question and answer in FAQPage JSON-LD; the Yoast and Rank Math FAQ blocks do this automatically. Reuse the most common questions as FAQ blocks on the property and weddings pages.

05

Correct the heading structure on property pages

Medium impact
Finding

Property pages contain multiple <h1> tags, with section labels such as "Amenities" also marked as H1.

Business impact

A single, clear H1 signals the primary topic of the page to Google, with sections stepping down to H2 and H3. An inconsistent hierarchy weakens topical relevance.

Recommended action Medium effort · developer

In the property template, set a single H1 of "{Property Name}, Luxury Villa in Kihei, Maui" and demote the section headings (Amenities, Location, Reviews) to H2.

06

Improve server response time

Medium impact
Finding

Time to first byte is approximately 2.1 seconds and full HTML load is approximately 2.4 seconds. The server is slow to respond before any content renders.

Business impact

Core Web Vitals are a ranking factor, and slow load times reduce booking conversions on mobile devices. A time to first byte below 0.8 seconds is the target.

Recommended action Medium effort · hosting / developer

Enable full-page caching (for example WP Rocket or a server-level cache), a content delivery network for images, and confirm the hosting plan provides adequate resources. Images are already in WebP format, so the focus is server performance and caching rather than media.

07

Strengthen on-page targeting for priority keywords

Quick win
Finding

High-value terms ("villa maui", "maui villas", "luxury vacation rentals in maui") rank on page two, primarily through the homepage, with no single page fully optimized for them.

Business impact

A focused page outperforms a general one. Modest on-page edits to existing pages can move these terms onto page one.

Recommended action Low effort · content team, ongoing

Develop the existing Accommodations page as the hub for "Maui villas and vacation rentals", and the Location page for Kihei and Wailea geographic intent. Incorporate the target phrase into the H1, opening paragraph, image alt text and internal anchor links from the homepage, and add a concise, genuine "About Kihei / Wailea" section to the Location page to reinforce local relevance.

08

Strengthen internal linking between pages

Quick win
Finding

Property and service pages exist but are not strongly cross-linked using descriptive anchor text.

Business impact

Internal links distribute authority and indicate to Google which page owns which topic, directly reinforcing the consolidation described in recommendations 02 and 07.

Recommended action Low effort · content team

From the Accommodations hub, link to each property using its target keyword as the anchor text (for example "oceanfront villa in Kihei"). Cross-link the Weddings, Events and Wellness pages, and link the Amenities and Activities pages back to Accommodations.
