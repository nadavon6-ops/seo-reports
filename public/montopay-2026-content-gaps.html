Methodology

1. SE Ranking Data Pull

Extracted all 33 tracked keywords, positions across Google IL/US/UK, search volumes, and CPC from SE Ranking Project 9702128. Competitive Research API not accessible with current plan.

2. Google SERP Validation

Ran 20 keyword searches in Google Incognito. Documented top 8 organic results per query, flagged 2026-published content, and tracked domain frequency across all SERPs.

3. Competitor Content Audit

Visited blogs of 12 competitor domains via WebFetch. Identified articles published in 2026 with schema datePublished verification. Catalogued 85+ verified 2026 articles across the competitive set.

4. MontoPay Content Inventory

Full crawl of montopay.com/resources and /category/articles/ across all 8 archive pages. Documented 71 articles. Verified each gap topic with site-specific searches.

5. Date Fact-Check

Every competitor URL in the final list was individually verified via WebFetch for schema datePublished markup. 7 corrections made from the initial draft based on date verification findings.

6. Gap Confirmation

Each of the 15 topics confirmed as absent or only tangentially covered on montopay.com via content audit. 10 complete gaps, 4 partial gaps, 1 exists with different scope.
