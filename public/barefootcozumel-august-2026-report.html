Insights & Recommendations

Executive summary for August 2026
✅ Positive trends
  • • Keywords in the Top 10 rose from 10 to 15 and those beyond position 20 fell from 9 to 5 over three months
  • • "dive shops cozumel" jumped 66→7 and "cozumel diving" 87→23 — the destination cluster is compounding
  • • "cozumel dive school" 13→4, "cozumel dive sites" 31→9, "cozumel reef" 23→8 — consistent gains on trip-planning terms
  • • "padi scuba diving" entered at #5 (390 volume) having never ranked before
  • • Brand searches grew 8.1% against a falling month — existing awareness is holding
  • • AI Assistant sessions rose 17.2% with 94 seconds average engagement, the highest of any channel
🎯 Recommended actions for September
  • • Fix the PADI certification levels page first — it drew 19,700 impressions in August but converted 0.13% of them into 26 clicks, the single largest gap on the site; its keyword also slipped 4→15, so the title, meta description and opening section all need rewriting
  • • Defend the three former #1s — "cozumel diving packages" (1→5), "Dive shop cozumel" (1→5) and "palancar reef" (1→2) all slipped; these are commercial terms and worth more than any informational gain
  • • Build a page for "diving center" (5,400 volume) — it has never ranked in any tracked timeframe, and nothing on the site currently targets it
  • • Lift the homepage — it earns the most clicks (156) but sits at an average position of 20, the weakest of any top page
  • • Convert the informational audience — the wildlife, water-temperature and scuba-tank articles bring the volume; adding clear course and package links inside them turns readers into enquiries
  • • Prepare for high season now — bookings for November-April are researched in September and October, so ranking gains made this month are what pay out in the peak
