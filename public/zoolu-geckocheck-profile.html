Read first

  • Zoolu is not in GeckoCheck yet. The account role is User and the app has no setup screen, so send section 01 to GeckoCheck support. The CSV files at the bottom are ready for a bulk import.
  • Two domains, one brand. The pet-store chain runs on zoolu.co.il and the vet clinic Zoolu Vet runs on zooluvet.co.il. Both must count as own site, otherwise every citation of the clinic is scored as a third party.
  • The bare name זולו is also the name of an unrelated family portal, zolo.co.il (games, calculators; zoolo.co.il and zooloo.co.il redirect to it). Searches such as טטריס זולו (590 a month), זולו סוליטר (480) and מחשבון אהבה זולו (110) are about that site. Keep זולו as a spelling, because it is the brand, but expect noise in brand-mention counts and check a sample of mentions after the first period. The Latin forms zolo and zoolo are the portal's own names and are not spellings of Zoolu.
  • The core set is 50 queries, 14 of them branded. The full set adds 37 more for a larger plan. No Search Console data was available for zoolu.co.il; demand comes from SE Ranking (volume of record) and Ahrefs (rankings and SERPs).
  • The five brand-line products use /food-brands/ URLs. A move of these pages to /brands/ is planned. Register the /food-brands/ URLs now and update the product URLs in GeckoCheck when the move goes live.
