Read first

  • This brand is not in GeckoCheck yet. The account role is User and the app has no setup screen, so send section 01 to GeckoCheck support to create it. The CSV files at the bottom are ready for a bulk import.
  • Zoolu (zoolu.co.il) is not on the competitor list and not a Mhaim spelling. The mhaim1.com terms of use name מ.ז. זולו פט וט בע״מ as the site owner, the same company that operates Zoolu, and the delivery page refers to רשת זולו. Both belong to the Milatin group. Set the brand up without Zoolu.
  • The core set is 50 queries (13 branded, 37 unbranded) across 8 categories and 11 products. The full set adds 33 more for a larger plan.
  • No Google Search Console data for this site. Demand comes from the SE Ranking tracked keywords and the SE Ranking Data API, which is the volume shown everywhere on this page. Ahrefs supplied positions for terms SE Ranking does not track. On the brand name itself Ahrefs estimates much higher demand than SE Ranking (30 a month); the SE Ranking figure is the one used.
  • Health topics are written without medical premises. Queries about therapeutic, hypoallergenic, dental and flea products ask how to choose or when to consult a vet. None states that a product treats or prevents a condition.
  • Never add זולו, חיים or איגואנה on its own as brand spellings. See the list under section 02.

Products

Mhaim sells thousands of SKUs, so products here are the flagship lines that have their own category page on mhaim1.com (each checked 24.09.2026 by its rendered H1) and measurable demand: Royal Canin (רויאל קנין 1,600 a month), Pro Plan (480), Taste of the Wild (480), Monge (מונג' אוכל לכלבים 590), Frontline (480), Seresto (סרסטו 320), KONG (קונג לכלב 390). Royal Canin and Monge appear twice because the site has separate dog and cat pages and each product must sit in one category; their spellings are split by pet so a mention is not counted twice. The two service products, home delivery and the Rishon LeZion branches, are what separates a local store with delivery from a pure online shop. Simba (סימבה, 1,900 a month) is left out: the term is mostly the Lion King character, and the Simba brand page ranks 16.
