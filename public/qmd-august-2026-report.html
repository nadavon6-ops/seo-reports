Insights & Recommendations

Executive summary for August 2026
✅ Positive trends
  • • Total sessions +24.0% (3,284 vs 2,648)
  • • Organic sessions held steady at 1,084 (+1.5%), engaging at 60.4% over 52s per session — the strongest of any channel carrying real volume
  • • AI-assistant sessions more than doubled — 18 to 37, and they engage at 64.9%, the highest rate on the site
  • • Referral +48.0% — 102 to 151 sessions
  • • Biometrix product page +40.4% — 47 to 66 clicks
  • • 3 keywords improved in France, 2 in the USA and 2 in the UK
  • • Vietnam nearly doubled — 33 to 60 clicks
🎯 Recommended actions for September
  • • Decide the market question first — rankings are tracked in the USA, UK and France while clicks come from India, Israel and Vietnam. Align the tracked set with commercial priorities before optimising further
  • • Treat the 135/180 AI Overviews as the primary battleground — with 0 keywords in the Top 10 anywhere, citation inside the AI answer is a more realistic near-term goal than a blue-link ranking
  • • Build genuine non-brand capability content — non-brand is 14 clicks against 364 brand; every head term in the tracked set is unranked
  • • Publish answer-shaped pages for the tracked head terms: a clear definition, specifications, standards and a comparison table — the format AI Overviews cite
  • • Investigate the Direct surge — 1,888 sessions at 32% engagement and 12s suggests campaign or untagged traffic worth attributing properly
  • • Define key events in GA4 — no conversions are configured, so enquiries cannot be measured at all
  • • Add UTM tagging so Direct and Referral can be separated from genuine type-in traffic
