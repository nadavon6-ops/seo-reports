5Images are not served in WebP or AVIFSite-wideText provided in the report
Source: Technical SEO Report, section 1
Every image on the site is still delivered as JPEG or PNG, including to browsers that advertise WebP and AVIF support. There is no picture element, no WebP variant in any srcset, and the image responses come back as image/jpeg and image/png. Any of the standard routes closes this: Cloudflare Polish with WebP enabled, since the site already runs behind Cloudflare, or an image plugin such as Imagify, ShortPixel or EWWW that converts the media library and serves WebP with a JPEG fallback. The instruction as sent:
"Compress all images, serve them in next-gen formats (WebP/AVIF), implement responsive srcset, and lazy-load below-the-fold imagery."
