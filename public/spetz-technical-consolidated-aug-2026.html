1Broken heading hierarchy in the site templates High prioritySite developerSpec & QA: retainer
The finding

The re-check confirms the issue raised in the meeting; it originates in the site templates themselves rather than in individual pages. Across a crawl of all 494 category pages, 299 of them (about 60%) carry more than one H1 heading, and on some pages buttons, form text and even phone numbers are marked as H1. On the content and article pages, 44 out of 100 sampled pages have two H1 headings. The homepage has a single, generic H1 ("רשימת תחומים" - list of fields) and no H2 or H3 at all.

Breakdown by template

  • Category pages (service_cat) - 299 of 494 pages with more than one H1. Example from the plumber page: five elements are marked H1 - "אינסטלטור", "אינסטלטור מומלץ 24/7", "חייגו עכשיו", the phone number itself, and "או מלאו פרטים". The template marks the entire call-to-action block as primary headings.
  • Content and service pages (service) - 44 of 100 sampled pages (out of 1,552) with two H1s: the term heading and the article heading (for example "מחירון תיקון מזגנים" followed by "כמה עולה תיקון מזגן ב-2025? מחירון מלא"). This includes the service pages written in May.
  • Homepage - a single H1 reading "רשימת תחומים" instead of a heading that represents the brand and the service, and no H2/H3 subheading anywhere on the page.
  • Static pages - 4 of 10 with a duplicate or empty H1 (for example empty H1 tags on the "מה זה ספץ" and "הצהרת נגישות" pages).
  • spetz.co.il (the main site) - no H1 at all; the main heading on the page is marked H3, followed by H2, H4, H5 and H6 in no particular order.

The impact

Headings are one of the primary signals Google and AI engines use to work out the main topic of each page. When a button or a phone number is marked as a primary heading, the search engine receives conflicting signals about what the page is about, which weakens the match between the page and its target search terms and also affects the way AI engines summarize and cite the page. This is the item with the widest reach on the list, because it affects hundreds of pages at once, and it is fixed with a single template change.

Solution and implementation

Recommended solution

One rule for every template: a single H1, which is the service name or the page topic, with every other element demoted a level or removed from heading tags altogether.

  • Category template (service_cat) - the category name remains the single H1. The marketing subheading ("אינסטלטור מומלץ 24/7") becomes an H2. The buttons, "חייגו עכשיו", the phone number and "מלאו פרטים" become presentational elements (div/span with exactly the same styling) - a change in the template file, with no visual change to the page.
  • Content page template (service) - the term name remains the H1; the article heading inside the content becomes an H2 (or is removed if it repeats the H1). Fixing this at template level is preferable; if the second heading is entered in the content itself, we will fix the leading pages through the editor.
  • Homepage - a new H1 that represents the service (for example "ספץ - בעלי מקצוע מומלצים בכל הארץ"), and converting the existing section headings ("שאלות נפוצות על המערכת", "איזורי שירות ספץ" and so on) to H2 in the code.
  • Static pages - removal of the empty and duplicate H1 tags (four pages).
  • spetz.co.il - setting the main heading on the page as H1 and ordering the hierarchy beneath it.

Acceptance check: after deployment we will re-run the full crawl of all pages and confirm: exactly one H1 per page, and zero buttons or phone numbers inside heading tags.

8The 2144.co.il domain is served outside Cloudflare Medium priorityCloudflare/DNS account access
The finding

As detailed in the August automated traffic report, the domain www.2144.co.il is served from an nginx server that is not behind Cloudflare. On re-check this month: its homepage does redirect (301) to service.spetz.co.il, but internal URLs under the domain still serve the site content itself. During the July-August wave of automated traffic, roughly 38% of the sessions entered through this domain because it bypasses the protection layer.

The impact

A protection-bypassing channel for bots, and duplicate URLs of the same content across two domains. The fix: moving the domain behind Cloudflare and applying a blanket 301 redirect for all paths.

Solution and implementation

Recommended solution

Add the 2144.co.il domain to the Cloudflare account in Proxy mode, and configure a blanket redirect rule: every URL under the domain (not only the homepage) 301-redirects to the corresponding URL on service.spetz.co.il. This closes the bot bypass channel and eliminates the duplication of content across two domains.
