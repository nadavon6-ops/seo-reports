Implementation notes & data decisions

  • Scope. Only the seven pages under the main-menu DIVE COURSES item are included. Cenotes and Packages sit under the separate Fun Dives menu and were intentionally excluded.
  • Single vs. multiple. Pages presenting one course use Course; pages presenting several courses use ItemList with each course nested as a Course item.
  • Divemaster is a single program with two pricing tracks (45,000 MXN from Advanced Open Water, 33,000 MXN from Rescue Diver) modelled as one Course with two Offers.
  • Cenotes excluded from the hub list. The /courses/ page shows a Cenotes card, but cenote diving is a guided dive (requires Open Water certification), not a training course, so it is not marked up as a Course.
  • Specialty courses have no individual pages — all six live on /specialties/ — so their ListItems nest a full Course without a per-course URL.
  • Prices verified from source. All amounts in MXN (Underwater Photography in USD) confirmed against the live page HTML. No prices were estimated or converted.
  • Ratings. The site's 4.9 / 2,197 review rating is a business-level rating and was deliberately not attached to individual courses, which would misrepresent it.
  • Provider. Every schema links to the site's canonical organization entity https://barefootcozumel.com/#organization (Barefoot Dive Center) with verified logo, phone and social profiles.
  • Validation. Each block was JSON-parsed and checked for required fields per type, absolute URLs and absence of placeholders. Recommended final step: run each through Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema.org Validator before publishing.
