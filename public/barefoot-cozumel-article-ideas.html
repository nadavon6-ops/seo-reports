Writing guidelines
Angle

Answer the fear question honestly in the first two sentences, then spend the article on what divers really encounter. The reader is nervous, not curious. Lead with reassurance backed by specifics, not with shark trivia.

Open with the direct answer

First sentence states plainly that yes, there are sharks in Cozumel, and that the ones you are likely to meet are nurse sharks and the occasional reef shark. This targets the featured snippet, so keep the answer inside 40 to 55 words in a single paragraph before any heading.

Structure

H2 per species you actually see (nurse shark, Caribbean reef shark, hammerhead in winter at depth, whale shark as a separate Isla Mujeres trip). Then an H2 on the safety record, an H2 on how to behave around a shark, an H2 on why divers hope to see one.

Must cover

Whether there have ever been shark attacks in Cozumel, why the marine park protects sharks, why numbers are lower here than in Playa del Carmen, the winter bull shark season being a Playa trip rather than a Cozumel one, and what a guide does when a shark appears.

The honest caveat

Say clearly that shark sightings in Cozumel are not guaranteed and are a bonus rather than the point of the dive. Overselling this is the fastest way to disappoint a guest.

Length and format

1,400 to 1,700 words. Species sections get a bold label then normal text. One short FAQ block at the end covering the three questions Google shows in People Also Ask.

Avoid

Do not repeat the bull shark trip content already published. This article should link to it rather than restate it, or the two pages compete for the same queries.

Length and format

1,300 to 1,600 words. A small table of dive profile against minimum wait. Short FAQ covering the diving-before-flying direction as well.

Do not invent a Barefoot policy number. If Barefoot enforces a specific cut-off on the last day, confirm it with the shop before publishing.

Must cover

The common two to twelve month and over-twelve-month thresholds operators apply, the specific skills that decay first (mask clearing, weighting, buoyancy, regulator recovery), what a pool or confined session covers, and the honest point that fitness and comfort matter more than the number of years.

The Cozumel argument

Warm water, high visibility and shallow reefs like Paradise Reef make this an unusually forgiving place to restart. Make that case concretely rather than as a slogan, since it is the reason to book here rather than at a local quarry.

The honest caveat

Some divers need more than one refresher session, and pretending otherwise sets guests up to have a bad first day back. Say that plainly.

Length and format

1,100 to 1,400 words. Keep it warm and reassuring in register. Short FAQ on whether the certification expires, which it does not.
