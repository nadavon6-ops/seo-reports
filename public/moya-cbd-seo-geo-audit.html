Top 10 Urgent Fixes

CRITICAL 1. Unblock /checkout/ in robots.txt

Issue: Checkout flow is blocked from crawlers, preventing proper e-commerce understanding.

Impact: Schema validation fails, conversion tracking impaired.

# Change in robots.txt: Disallow: /checkout/ ← REMOVE THIS Allow: /checkout/ ← ADD THIS

CRITICAL 2. Clean Sitemap Index

Issue: Stale entries from 2023 and 404 sitemaps referenced.

Remove: analiza-sitemap.xml, product-category-sitemap.xml, wpa-stats-type-sitemap.xml

HIGH 3. Enable Canonical Tags Site-Wide

Issue: Missing canonical tags on most pages creates duplicate content risk.

Fix: Yoast SEO > Settings > Advanced > Enable Canonical URLs

HIGH 4. Complete Product Schema

Missing: SKU, availability, aggregateRating, brand property

Impact: No rich snippets, reduced visibility in shopping results

HIGH 5. Add Image Alt Tags

All product gallery images lack alt text - impacts accessibility and image SEO.

HIGH 6. Enable Open Graph Tags

No social sharing metadata - poor previews when shared on social media.

MEDIUM 7. Fix Hebrew URL Encoding

URLs with Hebrew characters (/shop/כללי/) should redirect to romanized versions.

MEDIUM 8. Add Privacy Policy Page

Missing dedicated privacy policy affects trust signals and compliance.

MEDIUM 9. Implement hreflang Tags

Bilingual content (Hebrew/English) needs proper language targeting.

STRATEGIC 10. Build GEO/AI Visibility

Currently 25% - create Wikipedia entry, build authoritative citations.
