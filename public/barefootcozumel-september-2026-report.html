Clicks from Google held steady while GA4 recorded fewer sessions. Search Console recorded 1,002 clicks (+1.5%) on 134,046 impressions (-1.6%). Brand clicks (106), non-brand clicks from identified queries (222) and clicks from queries Google anonymises (674) add up to the 1,002 total. Clicks outside the identified brand queries, from named and anonymised queries together, came to 896 against 894 in August. That traffic was therefore flat; the lower identified non-brand figure means Google showed fewer of those queries by name.

The average position moved from 11.83 to 9.31. The gain is on desktop (16.41 to 10.54 in the 30-day comparison export), while mobile, which brings 642 of the 1,002 clicks, moved slightly the other way (7.77 to 8.13). At page level, about 31% of the improvement comes from impressions shifting toward pages that already ranked higher, and the rest from better average positions on the pages themselves.

GA4 sessions fell 39.0% (2,465 to 1,503), and the fall appears in Organic Search, Direct and Referral at the same time, while Google's own click count rose. The GSC-to-GA4 organic ratio moved from 0.59 to 0.95; both are inside the 0.5-1.5 range, but the shift is large. See the note under Traffic Sources.

Insights & Recommendations

Executive summary for September 2026
Positive trends
  • • Clicks from Google rose 1.5% to 1,002 while impressions dipped 1.6%, so the click-through rate moved from 0.72% to 0.75%
  • • Brand clicks grew 14.0% (93 to 106), and "barefoot dive center cozumel" now averages position 1.89 in Search Console
  • • The water temperature guide gained 49 clicks (105 in August 1-30 to 154), and the homepage rose from 153 to 178 over the same days
  • • Keywords in the Top 10 rose from 10 to 15 between the seven days to 15.06.2026 and the seven days to 15.09.2026 (SE Ranking, Google USA desktop, median positions)
  • • Clicks from the United States rose 9.9% to 520 (473 in August 1-30), the site's largest market
  • • AI Assistant sessions rose 26.5% to 43, the only GA4 channel that grew this month
Recommended actions for October

Each change can be made in WordPress (Yoast for titles and meta descriptions, Elementor or the post editor for headings and text). All copy describes only what the page already contains.

1. /cozumel-water-temperature-a-complete-guide-for-every-season/
This is the most-clicked article on the site (154 clicks in September). Its closing section, "Plan Your Cozumel Adventure with Barefoot", starts mid-sentence ("range of diving experiences...") and does not link to the course, package or cenote pages.
Change: Replace the first paragraph under that heading with the text below, linking the underlined phrases to /open-water-course/, /cozumel-dive-packages/ and /cenote-diving/.
ParagraphBarefoot Dive Center offers diving for every level, from your first certification to guided dives on Palancar Reef and Santa Rosa Wall. Beginners can complete the PADI Open Water certification in three days, and certified divers can choose a Cozumel dive package of three to seven days, from 6,000 MXN per person, with an optional cenote dive upgrade.
2. /cozumel-dive-packages/
The dive packages page improved from an average position of 35.45 (August 1-30) to 24.20, with 1,500 impressions and 22 clicks. Its main heading, "Dive & Excursion Packages", does not include "Cozumel", and the meta description does not show the prices that are on the page.
Change: Change the H1 to "Cozumel Dive Packages" and replace the meta description.
Meta description (146 characters)Cozumel dive packages from 3 days and 6 dives (6,000 MXN per person) to 7 days and 14 dives (14,000 MXN). Transport, lunch and park fees included.
3. /the-1-3-rule-in-scuba-diving-essential-guidelines-for-safe-and-efficient-dives/
The live title is visibly broken: it reads "Rule in Scuba Diving: Essential Guidelines for Dives", without the "1/3". The page drew 8,809 impressions in September (2,452 in August 1-30) at an average position of 5.89, but only 3 clicks.
Change: Replace the title and meta description. After saving, open the live page and check that the title still shows "1/3"; the slash was probably stripped by the SEO plugin or theme.
Title (57 characters)The 1/3 Rule in Scuba Diving: How to Plan Your Air Supply
Meta description (136 characters)The rule of thirds splits your air into three parts: one for the dive, one for the return and one in reserve. Includes a worked example.
4. /padi-certification-levels/
This page had the most impressions on the site, 15,439, but 12 clicks (0.08% CTR) at an average position of 10.69. The current title promises "10+ Courses", while the article describes four core levels and the beginner options.
Change: Replace the title and meta description so they match what the article covers. Separately, this article says the Open Water course takes 4 to 7 days from age 15, while /open-water-course/ says three days from age 12; the two pages should be aligned.
Title (59 characters)PADI Certification Levels: Open Water to Master Scuba Diver
Meta description (148 characters)The PADI levels in order: Open Water, Advanced Open Water, Rescue Diver and Master Scuba Diver, plus the beginner options and which level to choose.
5. /understanding-diving-flags-essential-safety-for-scuba-divers/
Lower priority. The query "how to use a dive flag" had 2,940 impressions and no clicks in September, but in a live US desktop search on 04.10 this page was the 23rd organic result, so it is not competing for that query yet. The page as a whole had 4,409 impressions and 4 clicks. The article has a "When and How to Use Diving Flags?" section that the title does not mention.
Change: Replace the title and meta description.
Title (60 characters)How to Use a Dive Flag: Diver Down and Alpha Flags Explained
Meta description (150 characters)Where to fly a dive flag, how far boats should stay away (100 to 300 feet) and how the red Diver Down flag differs from the blue and white Alpha flag.
6. /padi-meaning-what-does-padi-stand-for-in-scuba-diving/
Lower priority. Impressions rose to 8,774 (2,972 in August 1-30) at an average position of 10.91, with 3 clicks, and the query "padi meaning" had 748 impressions and no clicks. The current title already uses the meaning angle, so the expected gain is small; the change puts the exact question first.
Change: Replace the title and meta description.
Title (54 characters)What Does PADI Stand For? PADI Meaning in Scuba Diving
Meta description (145 characters)PADI stands for Professional Association of Diving Instructors. See when it started, what a PADI card lets you do and how PADI compares with SSI.
