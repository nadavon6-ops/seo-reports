The Villas

Three Private Pool Villas on One Quiet Property

Serene Mountain Villas is a small property in the Wok Tum area of Koh Phangan, Thailand. Three of its villas are 110 m² two-bedroom homes, each with two bathrooms and its own private saltwater swimming pool, sleeping up to four guests.

Every villa is detached and entirely yours: a private entrance, your own garden, terrace, balcony and patio, and a pool that is never shared. Inside, the kitchen is fully equipped, the living area has a sofa and dining space, and both bedrooms have a large double bed. Air conditioning and free WiFi run throughout.

The property sits in a quiet, green, residential part of the island, away from the busiest tourist areas. Local shops and restaurants are a short drive away, and a self-service laundry is nearby.

Guest rating: 5.0 out of 5 on Google, and rated Exceptional 10 on Booking.com.

What's Included

What Every Pool Villa Includes

Your Own Saltwater Pool

Each of the three villas has its own private outdoor swimming pool, filled with saltwater rather than chlorinated water. The pool is free to use, open all year, and there is no age restriction, and children are welcome in it.

Two Bedrooms, Two Bathrooms

Bedroom one and bedroom two each have a large double bed, and there are two full bathrooms, so a group of four never queues in the morning. Both bathrooms have a shower, bidet, toilet, towels and linen provided.

Please note: cots and extra beds are not available, and the maximum occupancy is four guests.

A Kitchen You Can Actually Cook In

The private kitchen is fully equipped with a stovetop, refrigerator, coffee machine, electric kettle, toaster and kitchenware, with a separate dining area. You can self-cater for the whole stay rather than eating out every night.

Living Space, Indoors and Out

Each villa has a living area with a sofa and seating area, and a flat-screen TV. Outside there is a private terrace, balcony, patio and garden with outdoor furniture, all looking onto the greenery.

Air Conditioning and Free WiFi

Air conditioning is fitted throughout, and free WiFi covers all areas of the property. All villas are non-smoking; there is a designated smoking area on site.

Getting Here

Getting Here and Getting Around Koh Phangan

The nearest airport is Samui International Airport, 48 km away. The property offers an airport shuttle service for an additional charge, and car hire can be arranged on site. Free private parking is available on the property and does not need to be reserved.

We recommend a scooter or a car to explore Koh Phangan comfortably. Most of the island's beaches, restaurants and viewpoints are best reached by transport.

Who It's For

Who the Villas Suit

The pool villas work well for couples, families and groups of friends looking for a peaceful island base. With two bedrooms, two bathrooms and a private pool, four people have room to spread out. Children of any age are welcome.

Note: guests aged 18 and over are charged as adults. Parties and events are not permitted, and quiet hours run from 21:00 to 07:00.

FAQ

Questions guests ask

How many villas have a private pool?

Three of the villas at Serene Mountain Villas have their own private pool. Each of those three is 110 m² with two bedrooms and two bathrooms and sleeps up to four guests.

Is the pool private or shared?

Private. Each of the three pool villas has its own outdoor swimming pool that is never shared with other guests. It is a saltwater pool, free to use and open all year.

How many people can each villa sleep?

Up to four. There are two bedrooms, each with one large double bed. Cots and extra beds are not available, so four is the maximum.

How many bathrooms does each villa have?

Two full bathrooms, each with a shower, bidet, toilet, towels and linen.

How big are the villas?

Each pool villa is 110 m² and is a detached, entire-property rental with its own private entrance.

Is there a kitchen?

Yes. Each villa has a private, fully equipped kitchen with a stovetop, refrigerator, coffee machine, electric kettle, toaster and kitchenware, plus a separate dining area.

Is there air conditioning and WiFi?

Yes. Air conditioning is fitted throughout each villa, and free WiFi covers all areas of the property.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00 to 22:00 and check-out is until 11:00. Please let us know your arrival time in advance.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free private parking on site, and you do not need to reserve it.

How far is the nearest airport?

Samui International Airport is 48 km away. We offer an airport shuttle service for an additional charge, and car hire can be arranged on site.

How far is the nearest beach?

Hin Kong Beach is 3.9 km away. Pleayleam Beach is 4.2 km, and Srithanu, Thong Sala and Nai Wok beaches are all about 5 km.

Is there anything to do nearby?

Phaeng Waterfall is 2.1 km away, Hollystone Relax Park is 2.5 km, and Jungle Flight Adventure Park is 10 km. Balance Specialty Coffee is 900 m from the property.

Do I need a scooter or car?

We recommend one. Most of Koh Phangan's beaches, restaurants and attractions are best reached by transport. Car hire can be arranged at the property.

Are children welcome?

Yes, children of any age are welcome, and there is no age restriction on the pool. Guests aged 18 and over are charged as adults.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed.

Can I smoke at the villa?

The villas are non-smoking throughout. There is a designated smoking area on the property.

Can I hold a party or event?

No. Parties and events are not permitted, and the property does not accommodate hen, stag or similar parties. Quiet hours are from 21:00 to 07:00.

Is laundry available?

There is a self-service laundry near the property.

What language do you speak?

English.

Where exactly are the villas?

92 81 Moo 3, Koh Phangan, Surat Thani 84280, in the Wok Tum area of the island.
