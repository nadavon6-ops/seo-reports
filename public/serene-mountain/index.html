The Villas

Three Private Pool Villas on One Quiet Property

Serene Mountain Villas is a small property in the Maduawan area of Koh Phangan, Thailand. Three of its villas are 110 m² two-bedroom homes, each with two bathrooms and its own private saltwater swimming pool, sleeping up to four guests.

Every villa is detached, with its own private entrance and its own pool. Nothing is shared with other guests.

The property sits in a quiet, green, residential part of the island, away from the busiest tourist areas. Local shops and restaurants are a short drive away, and a self-service laundry is nearby.

Rated 5.0 out of 5 on Google and Exceptional 10 on Booking.com. Hosted by Kalea, an Airbnb Superhost.

What's Included

What Every Pool Villa Includes

Your Own Saltwater Pool

Each of the three villas has its own private outdoor swimming pool, filled with saltwater rather than chlorinated water. The pool is free to use, open all year, and there is no age restriction, so children are welcome in it.

Two Bedrooms, Two Bathrooms

Bedroom one and bedroom two each have a large double bed, and there are two full bathrooms, so a group of four never queues in the morning. Children of any age are welcome, within a maximum of four guests per villa.

A Kitchen You Can Actually Cook In

Stove, refrigerator, freezer, microwave, coffee machine and kettle, plus pots and pans, oil, salt and pepper, plates, bowls, cups, silverware and wine glasses, with a separate dining table. Everything you need to cook a real meal, not just reheat one.

Hot Water and Full Toiletries

Hot water in both bathrooms, with shampoo, conditioner and shower gel provided, plus towels and a drying rack.

110 m², Entirely Your Own

Each villa is a detached 110 m² home with its own private entrance, a living area with a sofa, seating and a flat-screen TV, and its own terrace, balcony, patio and garden with outdoor furniture. No shared walls, no shared spaces.

Air Conditioning and Free WiFi

Air conditioning is fitted throughout, and free WiFi covers all areas of the property. The villas are non-smoking inside, and each one has its own balcony where you can smoke.

Self Check-In, Any Hour

Let yourself in with the lockbox. No waiting at a desk after a long flight.

Free Parking on Site

Free private parking on the property, no reservation needed, plus free street parking. Car hire and an airport shuttle can be arranged.

Long-term stays of 28 days or more are welcome. Message us for monthly rates.

Location

Where Serene Mountain Villas Is on Koh Phangan

Serene Mountain Villas is at 92 81 Moo 3, Koh Phangan, Surat Thani 84280, in the Maduawan area on the west side of the island. Phaeng Waterfall is 2.1 km away and the nearest beach, Hin Kong Beach, is 3.9 km.

Getting Here

Getting Here and Getting Around Koh Phangan

The nearest airport is Samui International Airport, 48 km away. The property offers an airport shuttle service for an additional charge, and car hire can be arranged on site. Free private parking is available on the property and does not need to be reserved.

We recommend a scooter or a car to explore Koh Phangan comfortably. Most of the island's beaches, restaurants and viewpoints are best reached by transport.

Who It's For

Who the Villas Suit

The pool villas suit couples, families and groups of friends looking for a peaceful island base. Children of any age are welcome, and there is no age restriction on the pool.

FAQ

Questions guests ask

How many villas have a private pool?

Three of the villas at Serene Mountain Villas have their own private pool. Each of those three is 110 m² with two bedrooms and two bathrooms and sleeps up to four guests.

How many people can each villa sleep?

Up to four guests. There are two bedrooms, each with a double bed, and two full bathrooms.

Can we bring children?

Yes, children of any age are welcome, and there is no age restriction on the pool. Children count toward the four-guest maximum, and guests aged 18 and over are charged as adults. Please note that cots and extra beds are not available, so if you are travelling with an infant you will need to bring a travel cot.

How big are the villas?

Each pool villa is 110 m², a detached, entire-property rental with its own private entrance, patio, balcony, terrace and garden.

Is there a proper kitchen?

Yes. Each villa has a full private kitchen with a stove, refrigerator, freezer, microwave, coffee machine and kettle, plus cooking basics, plates, bowls, cups, silverware and wine glasses, and a dining table.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is until 11:00. Check-in is self-service via a lockbox, so you can arrive on your own schedule.

Can I stay long term or monthly?

Yes. Long-term stays of 28 days or more are welcome. Message us for monthly rates.

How far is the nearest beach?

Hin Kong Beach is 3.9 km away and Pleayleam Beach is 4.2 km. Srithanu, Thong Sala and Nai Wok beaches are all about 5 km. Phaeng Waterfall is 2.1 km.

How do I get there from the airport?

Samui International Airport is 48 km away. We can arrange an airport shuttle for an additional charge, and car hire on site. Free private parking is available at the villa.

Do I need a scooter or car on Koh Phangan?

We recommend one. Most of the island's beaches, restaurants and viewpoints are best reached by transport, and car hire can be arranged at the property.

What are the house rules?

Maximum four guests per villa. Children of any age are welcome, with guests 18 and over charged as adults. Pets are not allowed, smoking is not permitted inside though each villa has its own balcony, and parties and events are not allowed. Quiet hours run from 21:00 to 07:00.
