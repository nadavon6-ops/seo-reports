Executive Summary

MontoPay has built a solid content library of 75 articles covering AR automation, AP portal management, invoice processing, DSO reduction, and AI in supplier portals. However, a comprehensive audit of 8 major competitors and 50+ Google searches reveals 15 significant content gaps where competitors are actively ranking and generating organic traffic that MontoPay is missing entirely.

Key finding: MontoPay's biggest content advantage — its focus on automating supplier interactions with buyer AP portals — is also its biggest untapped SEO opportunity. Most gap topics can be covered with a unique portal-first angle that no competitor currently offers, creating defensible content positioning.

AR Automation ROI Calculator: How Much Are Manual Portals Costing You?
500-1K /mo High Priority
Target Keyword
AR automation ROI calculator
Competitors Covering This
Serrala, Centime, Quadient, Auditoria, Emagia, Bill360 (6 have interactive tools)
Why MontoPay needs this: Bottom-of-funnel lead generation tool. 6 competitors have built dedicated interactive ROI calculators. MontoPay has zero. A portal-specific ROI angle (time saved on portal submissions, rejection reduction value, DSO impact) would be differentiated from generic AR calculators.
2
Accounts Receivable Trends 2026: The Rise of Portal-First AR
1K-2K /mo High Priority Trending
Target Keyword
accounts receivable trends 2026
Competitors Covering This
Forrester, Emagia, Quadient, Younium, Commercial Collectors
Why MontoPay needs this: Time-sensitive content. Forrester already published their 2026 AR trends report. MontoPay has a 2025 growth strategies article but nothing for 2026. Position "portal-first AR" as a defining 2026 trend that MontoPay pioneered.
3
B2B Payment Reminder Email & SMS Templates for Supplier Portal Invoices
2K-5K /mo High Priority
Target Keyword
payment reminder email templates B2B
Competitors Covering This
Gaviti, ChaserHQ, OpenPhone, Etactics, ProdigalTech (10+ results)
Why MontoPay needs this: Highest search volume opportunity on this list. Template content is extremely high-traffic, highly linkable, and has low production cost. MontoPay has zero template-based content. Portal-specific templates (e.g., "Following up on Coupa invoice #12345") would be unique in the market.

Dunning Management Best Practices: Automating Collections When Invoices Get Stuck in Portals
500-1K /mo High Priority
Target Keyword
dunning automation best practices

Unique angle: dunning when portal rejections or processing delays cause payment delays — different from traditional "customer didn't pay" dunning. Gaviti publishes both best practices AND software comparison content on this topic.
5
The Complete Guide to Supplier Onboarding Across AP Portals (Coupa, Ariba, SAP)

All existing content is buyer-side ("how to onboard your suppliers"). A guide from the supplier's POV ("how to onboard yourself to Coupa, Ariba, SAP as a vendor") would be industry-first content.

Order-to-Cash (O2C) Process Optimization: The Portal Automation Layer

Research Methodology

Step 1: MontoPay Site Audit

Full crawl of montopay.com including homepage, product pages, resources section, and all 75 published articles. Content catalogued by category.

Step 2: Competitor Discovery

10 targeted Google searches across AR automation, supplier portal automation, and O2C keywords. 20 competitors identified and ranked by organic visibility overlap.

Step 3: Competitor Content Audit

Direct audit of 8 major competitor blogs (HighRadius, Billtrust, Esker, Versapay, Tesorio, Gaviti, Kolleno, Upflow). 100+ articles documented with titles, URLs, and topics.

Step 4: Gap Validation

15 targeted Google searches to validate each gap topic. Checked MontoPay presence via site: searches. Documented top-ranking domains and competition density for volume estimation.

Step 5: Industry Trend Analysis

18 additional searches covering payment technology trends, agentic commerce, cross-border payments, and fintech content marketing for 2025-2026 context.

Step 6: Prioritization

Topics ranked by: search volume, number of competitors covering the topic, relevance to MontoPay's product, uniqueness of potential angle, and timeliness.

Note on search volume estimates: Volume ranges are estimated based on competition density, SERP quality, and the number of dedicated pages competing for each keyword. Without Ahrefs/Semrush data, exact volumes cannot be confirmed. Ranges are conservative estimates based on SERP indicators.
