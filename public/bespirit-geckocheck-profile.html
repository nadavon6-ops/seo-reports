Read first

  • The brand is not in GeckoCheck yet. Section 01 is the list of what to create, with the CSV files at the bottom for a bulk import.
  • One brand, two names. The site, its title and its schema call the college מכללת כרכור; BeSpirit is the website name (the domain, and the name the privacy policy gives the site). The operating company is המכללה לרפואה הוליסטית בע״מ. Create one brand with all 10 spellings, not two brands.
  • Never add כרכור on its own as a spelling. It is also the town (פרדס חנה-כרכור). The bare word drew 1,132 GSC impressions in 90 days but few clicks, and would count answers about the town as brand mentions.
  • The core set is 50 queries (15 branded, 35 unbranded) across 8 course-area categories and 15 programmes. The full set adds 33 more for a larger plan.
  • This is an education brand in a health-adjacent field. The queries ask about studies, certificates and careers. None of them assumes that a therapy treats or cures anything, so the answers measure the college, not medical claims.
