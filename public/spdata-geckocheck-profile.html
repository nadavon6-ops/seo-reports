Read first

  • sp.data is not in GeckoCheck yet. Section 01 lists what GeckoCheck staff need to create. The account role is User and the app has no setup screen, so send the whole profile to GeckoCheck support. The CSV files at the bottom are ready for a bulk import.
  • Qlik is not a brand spelling for sp.data. Qlik is the vendor's global brand, and other Israeli BI firms (Datamind, Logical, Tirosh, D.T.BIT) sell or implement Qlik too. Queries that name Qlik or Qlik Sense are set as unbranded. They stay in the set because they are where sp.data should be named: when an answer to who implements Qlik Sense in Israel names another firm, that is a real loss.
  • The old name still counts. sp.data traded as Qlik Israel until July 2026, and people still search for Qlik Israel (170 / mo in SE Ranking), so Qlik Israel and קליק ישראל are both brand spellings. The homepage title still reads sp.data (Qlik Israel לשעבר). qlik.co.il redirects to spdata.co.il and is listed as an own site, together with odoo.spdata.co.il.
  • The new brand name has no measured search volume yet. SE Ranking records no volume for sp.data, sp data or spdata. The spellings come from the site itself and its legal pages. Expect branded presence to be low in the first periods.
  • The core set is 50 queries (13 branded, 37 unbranded). The full set adds 33 more for a larger plan.
  • Israeli B2B data terms carry little measured search volume. Practitioners often search in English, and many Hebrew terms return no volume at all. A low or zero figure in section 06 does not mean nobody asks.
