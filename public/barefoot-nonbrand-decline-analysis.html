Key Takeaway

Despite overall organic traffic growing +51.1% YoY (GA4), 8 non-branded queries lost position in 2025 compared to 2024. The most severe declines hit core diving terms: "cozumel reef" and "cozumel dive sites" each dropped ~8-9 positions from page 1 to page 2. Priority actions: (1) rescue /coral-reefs-in-cozumel/ and /dive-sites/ with expanded, authoritative content, (2) refresh and expand /padi-certification-levels/ to defend against the certification cluster decline, (3) create a dedicated "dive shop" page to match local search intent.
