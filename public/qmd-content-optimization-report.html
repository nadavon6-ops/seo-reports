For the QMD marketing team
QMD — Content and Search Term Priorities
The changes worth making on qmd.net. They are numbered in the order we would carry them out, not in order of size: items 1 to 4 are quick, certain edits that can go live within a week, and item 5 is the largest driver of rankings but the one that needs writing time, so it should start in parallel rather than wait. Everything already implemented since the earlier reports has been checked against the live site and removed, and small changes that would not move anything have been left out. Every item names the exact page and the exact text to use.
1Give the 14 product pages a main heading14 pages · one batch
Fourteen product pages have no main heading. Each one opens with a second-level heading instead, so the page never states its own subject in the position search engines weigh most heavily after the title. These are the pages carrying the commercial search terms, including the vessel loops page at 3,330 searches a month. Keep the existing heading and change its level; do not add a new heading above it.
Who does it: whoever edits pages in Elementor. Click the heading, and in the left-hand panel change the setting called HTML Tag from H2 to H1. This changes only how the page labels the heading, not how it looks. Do not add a second heading.

3Put the homepage’s own search terms into the homepage1 page · biggest single term on the site
The homepage ranks for the QMD brand name and almost nothing else. The reason is simple: the phrase “medical device manufacturer”, searched 3,240 times a month and the single biggest term in QMD's keyword set, does not appear anywhere on the page. Neither does “contract manufacturing” as a service phrase. The changes below put both into the copy without altering the design or the brand voice.

5The product pages are too short to competemedian 249 words · needs writing
The seventeen product pages hold a median of 249 words of real content. The competitors ranking above QMD for these same terms, Freudenberg, Trelleborg and Spectrum, publish several times that. This is the biggest single reason strong products do not rank, and the only item here that needs writing rather than editing, which is why it should start now and run alongside items 1 to 4 rather than after them. The six pages below can be expanded immediately from material QMD has already published elsewhere on its own site. The remaining product pages need input from the product team on specifications, sizes and clinical use before anything is written, and that is the next piece of work to brief.
Who does it: whoever edits pages in Elementor. Click the text block named in the instruction and replace or add the wording shown.
