For the QMD marketing team
QMD — Content and Search Term Optimization
Every page of qmd.net was reviewed against the live site and against the search terms already identified in QMD's keyword research. Anything that has been implemented correctly since the earlier reports has been removed, so what is left is only what still needs doing. Scope is content, wording, headings, page titles and meta descriptions.
How to read this
  • Search volumes are the global monthly figures from the Keyword to Page Mapping. Terms marked research were never assigned to a page, and their volumes come from the full keyword research set. No new keyword research was carried out for this report.
  • Green text is ready to use as written. Copy it straight into Yoast or the Elementor widget named in the instruction.
  • Pages where nothing content related is still outstanding are not listed. That covers the contact page and the leadership page.
  • Heading level changes, where only the tag changes and the text stays, sit with the development team and are listed in the open action items. Heading changes that alter the wording are here.
  • The nineteen new pages and the eighteen article briefs from the earlier work are a separate workstream and are not repeated here.

Fourteen product pages still have no H114 pages
On all fourteen product pages the top heading is an H2, so the page has no H1 at all. The H1 is the single strongest on-page signal after the title, and these are the pages carrying the commercial terms, including the 2,830-a-month vessel loops page. In Elementor this is the HTML Tag dropdown on the heading widget, and the visual styling does not change.
The heading level clean-up is already specified and still open88 changes
Beyond the H1s, the previous report specified 88 heading changes across 25 pages: sections that skip from H2 straight to H5, card labels used as headings, table column labels marked up as H6, and calls to action marked up as H3.
