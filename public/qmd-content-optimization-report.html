For the QMD marketing team
QMD — Content and Search Term Priorities
Six things worth doing on qmd.net, in the order we would do them. Everything already implemented since the earlier reports has been checked against the live site and removed, and small changes that would not move anything have been left out. Each item is a single batch of work with the exact text to use.
1Give the 14 product pages a main heading14 pages · one batch
Fourteen product pages have no main heading. Every one of them opens with a second-level heading instead, which means the page never states its own subject in the place Google weighs most heavily after the title. These are the pages carrying the commercial search terms, including the vessel loops page at 3,330 searches a month. In Elementor this is the HTML Tag dropdown on the top heading widget, changed from H2 to H1. The visual design does not change.

3Put the homepage’s own search terms into the homepage1 page · biggest single term on the site
The homepage ranks for the QMD brand name and almost nothing else. The reason is simple: the phrase “medical device manufacturer”, which is searched 3,240 times a month and is the single biggest term in QMD's whole keyword set, does not appear anywhere on the page. Neither does “contract manufacturing” as a service phrase. The changes below put both into the copy without altering the design or the brand voice.

5The product pages are too short to competemedian 249 words · needs writing
The seventeen product pages hold a median of 249 words of real content. The competitors ranking above QMD for these same terms, Freudenberg, Trelleborg and Spectrum, publish several times that. This is the single biggest reason strong products do not rank, and it is the one item here that needs writing rather than editing. The pages below can be expanded straight away from material QMD has already published elsewhere on the site. The remainder need product-team input on specifications, sizes and clinical use before anything is written.
