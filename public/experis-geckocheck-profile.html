Read first

  • Experis is not in GeckoCheck yet. The account role is User and the app has no setup screen, so send section 01 to GeckoCheck support to create the brand. The CSV files at the bottom are ready for a bulk import.
  • One domain, three business lines. experis.co.il is IT staffing and tech jobs, experis.co.il/academy/ is Experis Academy (free career-switch tracks with placement) and experis.co.il/cyber/ is Experis Cyber. Experis Cyber sells managed cybersecurity services to organisations (SOC, EDR, CISO as a service, penetration testing, compliance). It does not run cyber training courses, so the Cyber categories and queries target security buyers, not students.
  • Experis Academy has exactly three live tracks (checked against the course sitemap on 24.09.2026): Software Development AI Native, AI/ML Engineer and BI. The automation track is not published and its old URL redirects to the course list. Add it as a product only once its page is live.
  • Never add אקספרס, Manpower or מנפאואר as brand spellings. אקספרס is the Hebrew word for express (1,300 searches a month, unrelated). Manpower, Manpower Care and ManpowerGroup are sister brands with their own sites and profiles.
  • The core set is 50 queries: 18 branded and 32 unbranded, across 7 measured categories and 14 products. The full set adds 36 more for a larger plan.
  • No Google Search Console access. Our key has no access to the experis.co.il property, so demand comes from SE Ranking (71 tracked keywords across the three projects, with Israeli volume) and Ahrefs organic keywords for Israel. Treat GSC-based checks as unavailable until the client grants access.
