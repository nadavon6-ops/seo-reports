Read first

  • GeckoCheck staff make the setup changes. The account role is User and the app has no setup screen, so send section 01 to GeckoCheck support. The CSV files at the bottom are ready for a bulk import.
  • Do not reword the 50 live queries. They carry the August 2026 history. They move into categories and get corrected stage, persona and branded tags; their wording stays exactly as it is.
  • All 50 live queries are marked unbranded, including the 5 that name לאומית. GeckoCheck is not recognising the Hebrew name. Until the spellings in section 02 are added, the Branded and Unbranded filters are wrong.
  • The core set stays at 50 queries. Leumit already runs 50, so every new core query is paired with a suggested retirement: 8 retirements, 8 replacements, listed at the end of section 07. The full set adds 38 more for a larger plan.
  • Health wording is deliberately neutral. No query asks for a diagnosis, a dosage or an outcome, and none contains personal health information. Keep it that way when editing.
  • לאומית is also an ordinary Hebrew adjective (national). Never add ביטוח לאומי, בנק לאומי or bare לאומי as spellings. Spot-check answer matches that come from forms like התוכנית הלאומית.
  • There is no Google Search Console access for leumit.co.il from our account. Search demand comes from SE Ranking (tracked keywords and the Data API) and Ahrefs. Ahrefs volumes are Ahrefs estimates and are labelled as such.
  • Symptom and condition keywords are left out of the keyword tables (BMI, sore throat, eczema, reflexology and similar). They bring search traffic but are general health information, not health-fund questions, and questions built on them would drift into diagnosis.
