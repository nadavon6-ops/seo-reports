Global Fixes — Apply to Every Page

These changes affect the entire site and should be done first. They unlock improvements for all individual pages.

Shorten the Site Name in Yoast

Current (38 characters wasted)
Makoa a Boutique Style Maui Resort
Recommended (14 characters)
Makoa Resorts

Yoast SEO > Search Appearance > General > Site Title. This frees up 24 characters in EVERY title tag for actual keywords. The phrase "a Boutique Style" adds zero search value.

Change Yoast Title Separator

Use | instead of -. It's visually cleaner in SERPs and is the industry standard. Yoast SEO > Search Appearance > General > Title Separator.

Noindex Taxonomy Pages (94+ pages of duplicate content)

Yoast SEO > Search Appearance > Taxonomies:
Property Amenities > Show in search results: No
Property Type > Show in search results: No
Property State > Show in search results: No
Property City > Show in search results: No
Property Flag > Show in search results: No

This removes ~100 pages with duplicate homepage metadata from Google's index. Single biggest technical fix.
