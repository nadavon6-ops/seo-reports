Read before rebuilding

  • Hebrew name variants are the whole game. In Otterly the brand was entered as "Cheryisrael" and scored 0 mentions. Adding צ'רי and צ׳רי took the same data to 8 mentions instantly. Enter both.
  • Two different apostrophes. צ'רי uses ASCII U+0027, צ׳רי uses the Hebrew geresh U+05F3. They are distinct strings and AI answers use both. Missing one loses mentions.
  • Give the competitors Hebrew names too. Latin-only competitor entries undercount everyone and flatter the client. After adding Hebrew, Hyundai went 14 to 31 mentions and Kia 17 to 31 on identical data.
  • Never add קרסו מוטורס as a brand alias. Carasso also imports Renault, Nissan and Dacia in Israel, so it would fire on competitors' answers.
  • Do not trend total brand mentions. Four of the 15 prompts contain צ'רי and almost always return a mention. Read Brand Defense separately from the contestable tags.
  • Country defaults to United States in bulk-import wizards. Set Israel before saving or every prompt is mis-targeted.

Domain traps caught during verification

  • kia.co.il is not Kia. It belongs to איגוד התעשייה הקיבוצית, the Kibbutz Industry Association. Kia Israel is kia-israel.co.il, importer Telcar.
  • These do not resolve at all: hyundai.co.il, mgmotor.co.il, byd.co.il, haval.co.il. The working ones are hyundaimotors.co.il (Colmobil), mg-israel.co.il (Lubinski), bydauto.co.il (Shlomo Motors).
  • Omoda-Jaecoo duplicates the separate Omoda and Jaecoo entries and scored 0%. In a fresh build, create only the two separate brands. The CSV above already omits it.

Instrument notes carried over from the Otterly build:
  - Claude has no ranking tracker in any tool. Measure it only via GA4
    claude.ai referrals and server-log ClaudeBot hits, and report it as
    "not measured" rather than as zero.
  - Report brand mention and domain citation as separate KPIs. On רכב סיני
    the brand is mentioned without the site being cited; one metric hides that.
  - Do not judge a Hebrew prompt by an "intent volume" score. In Otterly
    nearly every Israeli prompt lands in the lowest of four bands regardless
    of quality; the metric does not discriminate in this market.
