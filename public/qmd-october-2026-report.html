🎯 October: fewer brand searches, steady efficiency. GSC clicks fell from 910 to 807 (-11.3%) and impressions from 26,786 to 23,054 (-13.9%), while CTR rose from 3.40% to 3.50%. Brand clicks on identified queries fell by 69 (445 → 376), and clicks from India by 62 (237 → 175), so most of the drop is fewer people searching for the company by name. Non-brand held at 17 clicks (September 19). Position: the impression-weighted average moved from 6.4 to 7.0, but on the 278 queries that appeared in both months it improved from 8.2 to 7.8; the site average slipped mainly because of the query mix: brand impressions, which rank near the top, fell from 12,519 to 10,385. Organic sessions in GA4: 1,273 → 1,142 (-10.3%); GSC/GA4-organic ratio 0.71 (September 0.71). Total sessions fell by 906, of which 782 were Direct: Direct from Ireland and the Netherlands alone, the short-visit traffic flagged in September, fell by 859 sessions (GA4 by country).

    Title: Temperature Sensing Foley Catheter | Bladder Temperature Probe | QMD Biometrix
    Meta description: Biometrix 100% silicone Foley catheters with an integrated temperature probe for continuous bladder (core body) temperature monitoring, accurate to ±0.1°C. Sizes 6Fr to 18Fr.
  • • 746 impressionsAdd “central line kit” and “CVP kit” wording to the central venous catheter page (/product/biometrix-products/central-venous-catheters/)
    87 central-line queries reached this page with 746 impressions and 2 clicks in October (September 594), average position 14. The biggest are “central line kit” (114 impressions), “cvp kit” (107) and “cvc lumen” (78). The page already describes bedside kits and trays, but the title does not say “kit”. The approved H1 stays as it is.
    Title: Central Venous Catheter (CVC) Kits | Central Line & CVP Kits | QMD Biometrix
    Meta description: Biometrix central venous catheters and central line kits: single to quad-lumen CVCs for adult and pediatric patients, with bedside kits and trays.

    Title: Silicone Vessel Loops for Surgery | QMD Degania
  • • 283 impressionsName irrigation catheters in the urinary-catheter page title (/product/degania-products/urinary-catheters/)

  • • 84 clicksGive the Biometrix catalogue an HTML page Open since September

  • • 923 sessionsFilter the automated Direct traffic from the United States in GA4 Open since September

To check, open GA4 Explore, filter Session default channel group = Direct and Country = United States, and break the sessions down by browser, screen resolution and landing page: a single repeated combination with near-zero engagement points to automated traffic, which can then be excluded with a GA4 data filter.
