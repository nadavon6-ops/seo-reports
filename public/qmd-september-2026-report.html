Insights & Recommendations

Executive summary for September 2026
✅ Positive trends
  • • GSC clicks +22.5% (910 vs 743), with CTR up from 2.99% to 3.40%
  • • Organic sessions +17.4% (1,273 vs 1,084), engaging at 62.5%
  • • Biometrix product page +47.0% — 66 to 97 clicks, on 3,805 impressions (August 1,949)
  • • Leadership page doubled — 11 to 22 clicks, average position 12 to 5
  • • QMD is cited in an AI Overview — “temp sensing foley” in France
  • • AI-assistant sessions +24.3% — 37 to 46
🎯 Recommended actions for October
  • • Define key events in GA4 — no key events were recorded in September, so enquiries from search cannot be measured. Contact-form submissions, email clicks and catalogue downloads should be set as key events first
  • • Strengthen the existing temperature-sensing Foley page (/product/biometrix-products/temperature-sensing-tsc-foley-catheters/) — the Foley queries reached 1,370 impressions and 0 clicks, and the page itself 1,906 impressions at a 0.37% CTR. Searchers use “temperature probe” and “bladder temperature”, which the page title does not include. Recommended title: Temperature Sensing Foley Catheter | Bladder Temperature Probe | QMD Biometrix
  • • Fix the typo in the vessel-loops page title — it currently reads “Silicone Vessel Loops | QMD Degani”. Correct to: Silicone Vessel Loops for Surgery | QMD Degania. This page carries most of the non-brand clicks (1,343 impressions, 1.04% CTR)
  • • Check and filter automated traffic in GA4 — Direct grew by 1,196 sessions and is concentrated in the United States, Ireland and the Netherlands with visits of 11–28 seconds on average. If these are data-centre or bot visits, exclude them so the traffic figures reflect real visitors
  • • Give the Biometrix catalogue an HTML page — the PDF took 90 clicks in September (August 5). A PDF visit is not recorded in GA4; an HTML catalogue page with an enquiry form would make these visits measurable
