8 UX and SEO Notes

Hierarchy

Single H1, distinct H2s per major block, H3s inside Benefits, Capabilities, and Products. Source uses bold for everything. Enforce real heading tags.

Readability

Paragraphs of three lines or less. Bullet lists inside Benefits and Capabilities (already done, keep).

Above-the-fold (desktop)

H1, subtitle, primary CTA, and diagram visible without scroll.

Mobile

Capability grid in two columns, not three. Product cards stacked vertically with consistent height.

Intent alignment

Mid-funnel buyer evaluating platforms. Keep "Let's talk data" as primary CTA. Add a softer secondary ("Watch the demo" or "Download the data sheet") for top-of-funnel readers.

Schema (JSON-LD)

Product times three (Qlik Talend Cloud, Qlik Data Integration Client Managed, Talend Data Fabric). FAQPage with the real FAQs. BreadcrumbList for navigation.

GEO and AI search readiness

Add the short definitional passage near the top. Keep capability and product descriptions self-contained so an LLM can cite a single passage. Use plain language in the diagram caption.

Internal linking

Each Capability links to its product page on qlik.com. Each Product links to its dedicated page. Add a "Related solutions" block at the bottom.
