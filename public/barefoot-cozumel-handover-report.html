Handover Report

Barefoot Dive Center: SEO and AI Visibility Status and Action Plan

Site barefootcozumel.com Reporting period June to September 2026 Site reviewed September 2026
1

Where the site stands today

The content programme has built a real asset. The next phase is converting it. Barefoot is cited in more Google AI Overview answers than any Cozumel dive operator we measured, more than twice the next one, and the blog now carries four fifths of the site's search impressions and three quarters of its clicks. That authority is the platform everything in this report builds on, and it is why the recommendations below are additions rather than repairs. What sits on top of it is still to be built: the booking pages are short, the prices already on the site are not yet marked up for search engines, and the "diving in Cozumel" cluster is currently answered by a guide and the homepage rather than by a page built for that job. None of this needs a rebuild. It is the next stage of the same strategy, and the groundwork is already done.

Working

  • Google AI Overview presence Cited in 595 prompts, more than twice the next Cozumel operator
  • Editorial content authority 96 pages, 405,892 impressions in 92 days, positions 3 to 10 on the head informational terms
  • Tracked keyword positions Top 10 held at 19 of 27 across the quarter, top 30 gained one, none lost
  • Technical foundation 102 of 102 pages return 200, no noindex, AI crawlers explicitly allowed
  • Local pack presence Third of three listings in the map pack on "cozumel diving"

The opportunity

  • The commercial cluster is open 150 "Cozumel diving" queries already earning 31,078 impressions, with no page yet built to convert them
  • Conversion tracking to switch on The WhatsApp enquiry is the conversion; once it is a key event, every channel becomes measurable
  • Booking pages ready to build out 23 service and course pages sit under 400 words, so the upside per page is large
  • Prices ready to be marked up Five package tiers already published on the page, waiting on Service and Offer markup
  • AI citations to extend to the booking pages 68 of the 595 citations already reach a bookable page; the dive package prompts cite at slot 11 of 12

Keep measuring

  • Clicks easing through the quarter 912 to 752 across four equal blocks, average position 10.6 to 11.4, worth reading against the full history
  • Publishing cadence Twelve pages went live in July 2026; the cadence has been paused since 23 July
  • AI Overview link presence easing 876 in October 2025 to 636 in September 2026
  • Destination cluster unclaimed "Where is Cozumel" and "how to get to Cozumel" held by a competitor
  • Only one AI platform is measured ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini have no current reading; the last one is twelve months old

Clicks and sessions are differently defined, so they will never match, but a ratio in this range is what we would expect and gives no reason to distrust either export.

One thing to read before the action list. Several of the site's biggest pages rank well and draw few clicks: "padi certification" draws 12,297 impressions at position 7.4 for 7 clicks, "how to use a dive flag" 4,110 impressions at position 5.95 for none. These are questions Google now answers on the results page. Their return is the AI Overview citation and the brand exposure, which Barefoot is winning, not the click. They should be maintained, not optimised for click-through. A quarter spent chasing that CTR will not produce bookings.
The action plan
3High priority: do these first5 actions
1Build one page that owns "diving in Cozumel" and stop four URLs competing for itSEO
Page
/cozumel-diving-scuba-diving-in-cozumel-the-complete-guide/ as the target, with /, /beginner-dives/ and /dive-sites/ re-pointed away from the head term.
Finding
On a live United States result page checked on 9 September 2026, four Barefoot URLs appear for "scuba diving cozumel" at positions 15, 42, 76 and 88, and two appear for "cozumel diving" at 21 and 46. The best of them is a blog guide. Across the 150 non-brand "Cozumel plus diving" queries in the period, the site drew 31,078 impressions and 142 clicks, a click-through rate of 0.46%. Ahead of Barefoot on both result pages sit scubalifecozumel.com, scubatony.com, cozumeldiveschool.mx, sanddollarsports.com, diveparadise.com and blueprojectcozumel.com.
What to do
  • Promote the complete guide into the commercial page for this cluster. Put the package options, prices, boat and group size, and a booking call to action above the fold; keep the guide content below it so the informational rankings survive.
  • Add it to the main navigation and link it from the homepage hero. It currently sits three clicks deep.
  • Re-point the competing pages at their own intent: /beginner-dives/ to learning and first dives, /dive-sites/ to the sites themselves. Change the title tag and H1 so neither leads with the head term.
  • Keep the homepage on the brand and the offer, not on the generic term.
Why
This is the highest demand commercial cluster the site touches and no page is currently built to win it. Four Barefoot URLs competing on the same query is the first thing to resolve before expecting any of them to move.
2Track the conversions that already exist, starting with WhatsAppSEO and GEO
Where
Analytics property "Barefoot Dive Center Cozumel", the WhatsApp links sitewide, and the Elementor form named "Footer Form" that appears on /booking/ and across the site.
Finding
Every channel in the Analytics export shows 0 key events and 0 revenue for the whole 90 day period, while organic search delivered 5,149 sessions at a 66.5% engagement rate. The crawl shows why this matters: there is no third party booking engine on this site. The conversion path is a WhatsApp link to +52 984 208 6853, which appears 444 times across all 102 pages, plus the Elementor form. Neither is recorded as an event. Two separate Analytics measurement IDs are also firing on every page, G-Z82L6ZWCCV and G-0HNZP2XLD5, so the same traffic is being collected into two properties.
What to do
  • Mark the WhatsApp click as a key event. It is the primary conversion on this site and it is the single highest value change in this report.
  • Mark the Elementor form submission and any phone tap as key events too.
  • Establish which of the two measurement IDs is the property being reported on and whether the second is intentional. If it is a leftover, remove it; if both are wanted, label them so nobody reports the wrong one.
  • Once events are recorded, put a value on each so revenue by channel becomes readable.
Why
This is the cheapest item on the list and it upgrades every decision that follows. With the WhatsApp click recorded as a key event, the new team can attribute enquiries to channel and to page from day one, and re-sequence the rest of this plan against what actually converts rather than against search volume.
3Rebuild the twelve thin pages that take a bookingSEO
Pages
/fun-dives/ 446 words · /certified-divers/ 377 · /beginner-dives/ 358 · /discover-scuba-diving/ 375 · /intro-to-scuba/ 342 · /private-snorkel-tour/ 359. Then the six specialty pages: /deep-diver-specialty/ 83 · /peak-performance-buoyancy/ 93 · /enriched-air-nitrox/ 95 · /emergency-oxygen-provider/ 96 · /drift-diver-specialty/ 98 · /underwater-photography-specialty/ 114.
Finding
These are the pages that take a booking, and they are the shortest pages on the site, which is where the headroom is. Their average positions in the period run from 25 to 69: /beginner-dives/ at 58, /certified-divers/ at 69, /intro-to-scuba/ at 60, /discover-scuba-diving/ at 48, /fun-dives/ at 25. Together the 27 service pages produced 848 clicks against the blog's 2,518.
What to do
  • Take each to 700 to 900 words covering what is included, what it costs, how long it takes, the prerequisites, what the day actually looks like and where you dive.
  • Add four to six real questions per page. None of these pages currently carries an FAQ block.
  • The six specialty pages can be handled as one batch on a shared template; they are near identical in shape and each is under 120 words today.
Why
A 95 word page is not going to compete with operators running full course pages, and it cannot answer the questions a traveller asks before paying. This is the single largest body of unrealised value on the site.
4Add Service, Offer and Course markup to the pages that sellSEO and GEO
Pages
/cozumel-dive-packages/, /fun-dives/, /cenote-diving/, /private-snorkel-tour/, and the six PADI course pages.
Finding
We rendered all 102 pages in a real browser and read the structured data out of the live DOM. There is no Service, Product, Offer or Course markup anywhere on the site. /cozumel-dive-packages/ publishes a complete price list as plain text: five package tiers from 6,000 MXN for three days and six dives up to 14,000 MXN for seven days and fourteen dives, plus cenote upgrades from 900 to 2,100 MXN. None of it is marked up. LocalBusiness, AggregateRating and Review are present, correctly, on the homepage only. FAQPage is on 20 pages. The gap is specifically the commercial types.
What to do
  • Add Service with a nested Offer carrying price, priceCurrency and availability on the four booking pages, referencing the homepage organisation by identifier rather than repeating it.
  • Add Course with Offer and hasCourseInstance on the PADI course pages.
  • Do not add WebSite, Organization or LocalBusiness to these pages. They already exist on the homepage, and repeating them on inner pages sends conflicting signals for no gain.
  • Validate each type against Google's current requirements, not just for valid JSON.
Why
It is the clearest machine readable signal that these pages are bookable products with a price. The prices are already on the page in plain text; this makes them legible to a search engine and to an AI assistant rather than leaving them to be inferred.
5Move the AI Overview presence from the blog onto the pages that sellGEO
Pages
/cozumel-dive-packages/, /fun-dives/, /cenote-diving/, /courses/, and continued maintenance of /cozumel-water-temperature-a-complete-guide-for-every-season/ and /discover-the-top-22-things-to-do-in-cozumel-beaches-diving-and-must-see-spots/.
Finding
Barefoot is cited in 595 Google AI Overview prompts, more than any Cozumel competitor measured: sunsetcozumel.com 284, sanddollarsports.com 215, bluenotescuba.com 124, diveparadise.com 77, scubatony.com 64, scubalifecozumel.com 33, deepbluecozumel.com 32. That lead is real and it is the most valuable thing in this handover. But 135 of those citations come from a single page, the water temperature guide, and only 66 of the 595 prompts are commercial in shape. On the dive package prompts Barefoot is cited at slot 11 or 12 out of 12. Link presence has eased from 876 in October 2025 to 636 in September 2026.
What to do
  • Add a short quotable answer block near the top of each commercial page, in complete sentences: what it costs, how long it runs, group size, what is included, who it suits. Generative answers quote self-contained sentences, not tables and not bullet fragments.
  • Add FAQ blocks to /cozumel-dive-packages/, /fun-dives/ and /cenote-diving/, none of which has one.
  • Keep the water temperature guide and the things to do guide updated monthly. They carry the citations that hold the whole position up.
  • Re-measure monthly. The decline from 876 to 636 is the number to watch.
Why
The AI channel already sends traffic and it is the most engaged traffic on the site: 96 sessions at a 70.8% engagement rate and 9.1 events per session, higher on both counts than organic search. Of the 595 AI Overview citations, 68 land on a page that can take a booking and the other 527 land on guides.

6Claim the Cozumel destination cluster, currently held by a competitorSEO and GEO
Page
New page, suggested /cozumel-for-divers/ or a rework of /diving-in-cozumel-reserve/, which is 292 words today.
Finding
"Where is cozumel" (660 a month), "how to get to cozumel" (480) and roughly a dozen close variants have no Barefoot presence at all. We checked both Search Console and the ranking database and found nothing. deepbluecozumel.com holds positions 4 to 9 across the cluster with a single page, deepbluecozumel.com/discover-cozumel/, and sanddollarsports.com holds position 2 on "how to get to cozumel".
What to do
  • One page answering the practical questions: where Cozumel is, how to reach it from Cancun and from the cruise pier, how big the island is, when to come, what it costs to get around.
  • Answer each question in its first sentence, then expand. This is the shape generative answers quote.
  • Link it to the dive pages, not the other way round. It is a top of funnel entry point.
Why
Roughly 1,100 searches a month of pure destination intent, held by a direct competitor with one page. It is also the kind of question AI assistants answer constantly, which makes it a citation opportunity as much as a ranking one.
7Give snorkelling and whale sharks commercial pages instead of blog postsSEO
Pages
/private-snorkel-tour/ and /swimming-with-whale-sharks-in-2024/
Finding
Snorkelling is open ground. "Best snorkeling in cozumel" (480 a month) and "cozumel snorkeling" (360) return no Barefoot result, and no Cozumel operator we measured holds a top 30 position on either, even though scubalifecozumel.com and deepbluecozumel.com both run a snorkelling page. Barefoot's only snorkelling page is /private-snorkel-tour/, 359 words, average position 47. Whale sharks are a different problem. Barefoot already ranks at 13 for "whale shark tour cozumel" (590 a month), ahead of diveparadise.com at 14, and at 10 for "cozumel whale shark excursions" (590). It ranks there with a blog post at /swimming-with-whale-sharks-in-2024/, whose URL carries a stale year and which is not set up to take a booking. diveparadise.com converts the same demand through a commercial page at diveparadise.com/swim-whale-sharks/.
What to do
  • Build /cozumel-snorkeling/ as a commercial page covering the sites, the seasons, what is included and the price, and keep the private tour page as the booking option beneath it.
  • Move the whale shark post to a URL without a year and redirect the old one with a single 301. Then rebuild it as a bookable page with season dates, price and what the day involves. Confirm the redirect resolves in one hop before it goes live.
Why
Around 1,900 searches a month across the two topics. On snorkelling nobody has claimed it, which makes it the cheapest new page on this list. On whale sharks the ranking already exists and only the page type is wrong.
8Build a page for divers arriving on a cruise shipSEO
Page
New page, plus a link from /insiders-guide-to-the-cozumel-cruise-port-and-island-escapes/

The cruise port guide drew 9,215 impressions at average position 17.9 in the period, and Barefoot is cited in AI Overviews on "port schedule cozumel" at slot 1 and "cozumel mexico beaches near cruise port" at slot 6. 9Repair two broken FAQ schema blocksSEO
Pages
/boat-diving-in-cozumel-what-you-need-to-know/
/coral-reefs-in-cozumel/
Finding
Both pages carry a second JSON-LD block, separate from the Yoast one, that fails to parse. On the boat diving page the JSON contains raw line breaks inside string values, starting at "@type\n ":"FAQPage". On the coral reefs page a quoted phrase, "Devil's Throat,", is inserted into an answer without escaping the double quotes. Both fail in the rendered browser DOM, not just in the source, so Google is not currently able to read the FAQ markup on these two pages.
What to do
  • Escape the inner quotes as \" and strip the raw newlines from the JSON string values. The visible page content does not change.
  • Re-check both in the Rich Results Test after the fix.
  • Whatever inserts these blocks should be checked, since the same fault will recur on the next page that quotes a phrase.
Why
A few characters of escaping brings the FAQ markup on both pages back into play. Short fix, definite result.
10Clean up the sitemap and one missing canonicalSEO
Pages
/courses/ and /dive-master-internship-old/
Finding
/courses/ returns 200, is linked five times from the homepage, and drew 1,173 impressions at average position 32 in the period. It is absent from the XML sitemap and it has no rel="canonical" tag, which every other page on the site has. /dive-master-internship-old/ is in the sitemap, canonicalises to /dive-master/ and carries an identical title tag to it.
What to do
  • Add /courses/ to the sitemap and give it a self-referencing canonical. Check the Yoast settings on that page, since something is suppressing both.
  • Remove /dive-master-internship-old/ from the sitemap and 301 it to /dive-master/.
Why
/courses/ is the hub for the training business and it is the one page the site is not telling Google about properly.

What only the outgoing team can hand over

This report covers what can be measured from the outside. The following cannot be, and should be transferred in the handover conversation rather than looked for in this document:

  • Credentials and account ownership. Deliberately not in this document, since the report is a shareable link. Transfer them separately.
  • Client contacts and who decides what. Who signs off content, who can change the site, who answers the WhatsApp.
  • The client's own commercial priorities. Whether the goal is more PADI course enrolments, more multi day packages, more same day cruise divers or higher season fill. This report ranks by search opportunity; that ranking should be re-sorted against what the business actually wants to sell.
  • What has already been tried and did not work, and why publishing stopped on 23 July 2026.
  • Season and capacity constraints. How many divers the operation can take, when it is already full, and which months do not need more demand.
  • Any commitments already made to the client that this report does not know about.

What was not covered

Backlinks and off-site authority were not analysed. Page speed and Core Web Vitals were not measured. Neither was in the brief for this report, and both are worth a look early in the new engagement, particularly given the number of images on the course pages.
