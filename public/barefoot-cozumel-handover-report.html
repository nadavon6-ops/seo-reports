Handover Report

Barefoot Dive Center: SEO and AI Visibility Status and Action Plan

Site barefootcozumel.com Reporting period June to September 2026 Site reviewed September 2026
1

Where the site stands today

The site is not in decline, but its search visibility and its commercial pages have come apart. Barefoot ranks and is cited more widely than any other Cozumel dive operator we measured, and almost all of that reach sits on the blog. The pages that take a booking rank on page three and below, and the one cluster that matters commercially, "diving in Cozumel", is served by a blog post and the homepage rather than by a page built to win it. Nothing here needs a rebuild. It needs the existing authority pointed at the pages that sell.

Working

  • Google AI Overview presence Cited in 595 prompts, ahead of every Cozumel competitor measured
  • Editorial content authority 96 pages, 405,892 impressions in 90 days, positions 3 to 10 on the head informational terms
  • Tracked keyword positions Top 10 held at 19 of 27 across the quarter, top 3 rose from 5 to 6
  • Technical foundation 102 of 102 pages return 200, no noindex, AI crawlers explicitly allowed
  • Local pack presence Barefoot appears in the map pack on "cozumel diving"

Needs attention

  • The commercial cluster 150 "Cozumel diving" queries, 31,078 impressions, 142 clicks, 0.46% CTR
  • No conversion tracking GA4 records 0 key events and 0 revenue on every channel for the full period
  • Thin booking pages 23 service and course pages under 400 words, six of them under 120
  • No product or service markup Prices published as plain text, no Offer schema anywhere on the site
  • AI citations sit on the blog Only 66 of 595 cited prompts are commercial, and those cite at slot 11 of 12

Watch

  • Clicks easing through the quarter 912 to 752 across four equal blocks, average position 10.6 to 11.4
  • Publishing stopped Last page published 23 July 2026, twelve went live that month
  • AI Overview link presence easing 876 in October 2025 to 636 in September 2026
  • Destination cluster unclaimed "Where is Cozumel" and "how to get to Cozumel" held by a competitor

Search Console recorded 3,351 clicks against 5,149 organic sessions, a ratio of 0.65, which is inside the normal band and means the two data sets agree.

One thing to read before the action list. Several of the site's biggest pages rank well and are barely clicked: "padi certification" draws 12,297 impressions at position 7.4 for 7 clicks, "how to use a dive flag" 4,110 impressions at position 5.95 for none. These are questions Google now answers on the results page. Their return is the AI Overview citation and the brand exposure, which Barefoot is winning, not the click. They should be maintained, not optimised for click-through. A quarter spent chasing that CTR will not produce bookings.
The action plan
3High priority: do these first5 actions
1Build one page that owns "diving in Cozumel" and stop four URLs competing for itSEO
Page
/cozumel-diving-scuba-diving-in-cozumel-the-complete-guide/ as the target, with /, /beginner-dives/ and /dive-sites/ re-pointed away from the head term.
Finding
On a live United States result page checked on 9 September 2026, four Barefoot URLs appear for "scuba diving cozumel" at positions 15, 42, 76 and 88, and two appear for "cozumel diving" at 21 and 46. The best of them is a blog guide. Across the 150 non-brand "Cozumel plus diving" queries in the period, the site drew 31,078 impressions and 142 clicks, a click-through rate of 0.46%. Ahead of Barefoot on both result pages sit scubalifecozumel.com, scubatony.com, cozumeldiveschool.mx, sanddollarsports.com, diveparadise.com and blueprojectcozumel.com, each with a page named for the term.
What to do
  • Promote the complete guide into the commercial page for this cluster. Put the package options, prices, boat and group size, and a booking call to action above the fold; keep the guide content below it so the informational rankings survive.
  • Add it to the main navigation and link it from the homepage hero. It currently sits three clicks deep.
  • Re-point the competing pages at their own intent: /beginner-dives/ to learning and first dives, /dive-sites/ to the sites themselves. Change the title tag and H1 so neither leads with the head term.
  • Keep the homepage on the brand and the offer, not on the generic term.
Why
This is the highest demand commercial cluster the site touches and no page is currently built to win it. Four URLs splitting the same query is the reason the best one sits at 15 rather than in the top ten.
2Turn on conversion tracking in Analytics before anything is measured against itSEO and GEO
Page
Analytics property "Barefoot Dive Center Cozumel", plus the booking flow on /booking/ and every course and package page.
Finding
Every channel in the Analytics export shows 0 key events and 0 revenue for the whole 90 day period. Organic search delivered 5,149 sessions with a 66.5% engagement rate and nothing downstream is recorded. /booking/ is 28 words of content and carries 77 impressions.
What to do
  • Mark the enquiry form submission, the outbound click to the booking system, the WhatsApp click and the phone tap as key events.
  • If booking completes on an external system, add that domain as a referral exclusion so the session is not re-attributed away from organic, and tag the outbound link.
  • Set a value on each so revenue by channel becomes readable.
Why
Nobody can currently say what any of this traffic is worth, which means every priority decision after this one is an estimate. It is the cheapest item on the list and it changes the quality of every decision that follows.
3Rebuild the twelve thinnest booking pagesSEO
Pages
/fun-dives/ 446 words · /certified-divers/ 377 · /beginner-dives/ 358 · /discover-scuba-diving/ 375 · /intro-to-scuba/ 342 · /private-snorkel-tour/ 359. Then the six specialty pages: /deep-diver-specialty/ 83 · /peak-performance-buoyancy/ 93 · /enriched-air-nitrox/ 95 · /emergency-oxygen-provider/ 96 · /drift-diver-specialty/ 98 · /underwater-photography-specialty/ 114.
Finding
These are the pages that take a booking and they are the weakest pages on the site. Their average positions in the period run from 25 to 69: /beginner-dives/ at 58, /certified-divers/ at 69, /intro-to-scuba/ at 60, /discover-scuba-diving/ at 48, /fun-dives/ at 25. Together the 27 service pages produced 848 clicks against the blog's 2,518.
What to do
  • Take each to 700 to 900 words covering what is included, what it costs, how long it takes, the prerequisites, what the day actually looks like and where you dive.
  • Add four to six real questions per page. None of these pages currently carries an FAQ block.
  • The six specialty pages can be handled as one batch on a shared template; they are near identical in shape and each is under 120 words today.
Why
A 95 word page cannot rank against competitors running full course pages, and it cannot answer the questions a traveller asks before paying. This is the single largest body of unrealised value on the site.
4Add Service, Offer and Course markup to the pages that sellSEO and GEO
Pages
/cozumel-dive-packages/, /fun-dives/, /cenote-diving/, /private-snorkel-tour/, and the six PADI course pages.
Finding
We rendered all 102 pages in a real browser and read the structured data out of the live DOM. There is no Service, Product, Offer or Course markup anywhere on the site. /cozumel-dive-packages/ publishes real prices, 6,000 MXN for three days and six dives and 8,000 MXN for four days and eight dives, as plain text with nothing marked up. LocalBusiness, AggregateRating and Review are present, correctly, on the homepage only. FAQPage is on 20 pages. The gap is specifically the commercial types.
What to do
  • Add Service with a nested Offer carrying price, priceCurrency and availability on the four booking pages, referencing the homepage organisation by identifier rather than repeating it.
  • Add Course with Offer and hasCourseInstance on the PADI course pages.
  • Do not add WebSite, Organization or LocalBusiness to these pages. They already exist on the homepage and duplicating them causes more harm than the addition is worth.
  • Validate each type against Google's current requirements, not just for valid JSON.
Why
This is the only signal that tells a search engine or an AI assistant that these pages are bookable products with a price. Price and availability are exactly what generative answers pull when a traveller asks what diving in Cozumel costs.
5Move the AI Overview presence from the blog onto the pages that sellGEO
Pages
/cozumel-dive-packages/, /fun-dives/, /cenote-diving/, /courses/, and continued maintenance of /cozumel-water-temperature-a-complete-guide-for-every-season/ and /discover-the-top-22-things-to-do-in-cozumel-beaches-diving-and-must-see-spots/.
Finding
Barefoot is cited in 595 Google AI Overview prompts, more than any Cozumel competitor measured: sunsetcozumel.com 284, sanddollarsports.com 215, bluenotescuba.com 124, diveparadise.com 77, scubatony.com 64, scubalifecozumel.com 33, deepbluecozumel.com 32. That lead is real and it is the most valuable thing in this handover. But 117 of those citations come from a single page, the water temperature guide, and only 66 of the 595 prompts are commercial in shape. On the dive package prompts Barefoot is cited at slot 11 or 12 out of 12. Link presence has eased from 876 in October 2025 to 636 in September 2026.
What to do
  • Add a short quotable answer block near the top of each commercial page, in complete sentences: what it costs, how long it runs, group size, what is included, who it suits. Generative answers quote self-contained sentences, not tables and not bullet fragments.
  • Add FAQ blocks to /cozumel-dive-packages/, /fun-dives/ and /cenote-diving/, none of which has one.
  • Keep the water temperature guide and the things to do guide updated monthly. They carry the citations that hold the whole position up.
  • Re-measure monthly. The decline from 876 to 636 is the number to watch.
Why
The AI channel already sends traffic and it is the most engaged traffic on the site: 96 sessions at a 70.8% engagement rate and 9.1 events per session, higher on both counts than organic search. The citations currently land on pages that cannot take a booking.

6Claim the Cozumel destination cluster, currently held by a competitorSEO and GEO
Page
New page, suggested /cozumel-for-divers/ or a rework of /diving-in-cozumel-reserve/, which is 292 words today.
Finding
"Where is cozumel" (660 a month), "how to get to cozumel" (480) and roughly a dozen close variants have no Barefoot presence at all. We checked both Search Console and the ranking database and found nothing. deepbluecozumel.com holds positions 4 to 9 across the cluster with a single page, deepbluecozumel.com/discover-cozumel/, and sanddollarsports.com holds position 2 on "how to get to cozumel".
What to do
  • One page answering the practical questions: where Cozumel is, how to reach it from Cancun and from the cruise pier, how big the island is, when to come, what it costs to get around.
  • Answer each question in its first sentence, then expand. This is the shape generative answers quote.
  • Link it to the dive pages, not the other way round. It is a top of funnel entry point.
Why
Roughly 1,100 searches a month of pure destination intent, held by a direct competitor with one page. It is also the kind of question AI assistants answer constantly, which makes it a citation opportunity as much as a ranking one.
7Give snorkelling and whale sharks commercial pages instead of blog postsSEO
Pages
/private-snorkel-tour/ and /swimming-with-whale-sharks-in-2024/
Finding
Snorkelling is open ground. "Best snorkeling in cozumel" (480 a month) and "cozumel snorkeling" (360) return no Barefoot result, and no Cozumel operator we measured holds a top 30 position on either, even though scubalifecozumel.com and deepbluecozumel.com both run a snorkelling page. Barefoot's only snorkelling page is /private-snorkel-tour/, 359 words, average position 47. Whale sharks are a different problem. Barefoot already ranks at 13 for "whale shark tour cozumel" (590 a month), ahead of diveparadise.com at 14, and at 10 for "cozumel whale shark excursions" (590). It ranks there with a blog post at /swimming-with-whale-sharks-in-2024/, whose URL carries a stale year and which cannot take a booking. diveparadise.com converts the same demand through a commercial page at diveparadise.com/swim-whale-sharks/.
What to do
  • Build /cozumel-snorkeling/ as a commercial page covering the sites, the seasons, what is included and the price, and keep the private tour page as the booking option beneath it.
  • Move the whale shark post to a URL without a year and redirect the old one with a single 301. Then rebuild it as a bookable page with season dates, price and what the day involves. Confirm the redirect resolves in one hop before it goes live.
Why
Around 1,900 searches a month across the two topics. On snorkelling nobody has claimed it, which makes it the cheapest new page on this list. On whale sharks the ranking already exists and only the page type is wrong.
8Build a page for divers arriving on a cruise shipSEO
Page
New page, plus a link from /insiders-guide-to-the-cozumel-cruise-port-and-island-escapes/

The cruise port guide drew 9,215 impressions at average position 17.9 in the period, and Barefoot is cited in AI Overviews on "port schedule cozumel" at slot 1 and "cozumel mexico beaches near cruise port" at slot 6. 9Repair two broken FAQ schema blocksSEO
Pages
/boat-diving-in-cozumel-what-you-need-to-know/
/coral-reefs-in-cozumel/
Finding
Both pages carry a second JSON-LD block, separate from the Yoast one, that fails to parse. On the boat diving page the JSON contains raw line breaks inside string values, starting at "@type\n ":"FAQPage". On the coral reefs page a quoted phrase, "Devil's Throat,", is inserted into an answer without escaping the double quotes. Both fail in the rendered browser DOM, not just in the source, so the FAQ markup on these two pages is dead as far as Google is concerned.
What to do
  • Escape the inner quotes as \" and strip the raw newlines from the JSON string values. The visible page content does not change.
  • Re-check both in the Rich Results Test after the fix.
  • Whatever inserts these blocks should be checked, since the same fault will recur on the next page that quotes a phrase.
Why
Two pages are carrying FAQ markup that does nothing. It is a short fix with a definite result.
10Clean up the sitemap and one missing canonicalSEO
Pages
/courses/ and /dive-master-internship-old/
Finding
/courses/ returns 200, is linked five times from the homepage, and drew 1,173 impressions at average position 32 in the period. It is absent from the XML sitemap and it has no rel="canonical" tag, which every other page on the site has. /dive-master-internship-old/ is in the sitemap, canonicalises to /dive-master/ and carries an identical title tag to it.
What to do
  • Add /courses/ to the sitemap and give it a self-referencing canonical. Check the Yoast settings on that page, since something is suppressing both.
  • Remove /dive-master-internship-old/ from the sitemap and 301 it to /dive-master/.
Why
/courses/ is the hub for the training business and it is the one page the site is not telling Google about properly.

Pages

Two things the new team should know before trusting a number

  • The tracked keyword tool and Search Console disagree on the commercial terms. The project tracker reports Barefoot at position 2 for "scuba diving cozumel" and 7 for "cozumel scuba diving". Search Console reports 23.8 and 21.7 for the same terms over the period, and a live United States result page on 9 September 2026 put the best Barefoot URL at 15 and 21. Two independent instruments agree the site is on page two, so this report uses Search Console and the live check for every commercial claim. The tracker's figures on the head terms should not be quoted to the client without a live check first.
  • Search volume figures disagree between instruments too. The keyword database returns 40 a month for "cozumel diving" while Search Console recorded 2,178 impressions for it in 90 days. Where Search Console has data it is the better read on demand, and this report uses impressions rather than volume wherever the site already ranks. Volume figures appear only for terms the site does not rank for at all, where Search Console has nothing to say.

Access the new team will need

  • Google Search Console for barefootcozumel.com. This report used a client supplied export; the property is not shared with our account, so nothing here required or received ongoing access.
  • Google Analytics 4, property "Barefoot Dive Center Cozumel". Same position, export only.
  • WordPress admin, including Yoast SEO. Actions 4, 9, 10 and 11 all run through it.
  • Google Tag Manager, which is live on all 102 pages and is where action 2 will be implemented.
  • Google Business Profile. Barefoot holds a map pack position on "cozumel diving" and that surface is not covered by this report.
  • A rank tracking and AI visibility subscription of the new team's own. The tracked keyword set and the AI Mode prompt list described above sit in our SE Ranking account and will not transfer.

What was not covered

Backlinks and off-site authority were not analysed. Page speed and Core Web Vitals were not measured. Neither was in the brief for this report, and both are worth a look early in the new engagement, particularly given the number of images on the course pages.
