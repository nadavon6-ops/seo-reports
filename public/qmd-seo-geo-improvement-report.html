NC Digital QMD SEO Improvement Report March 2026

QMD.net — SEO Improvement Report

Keyword-to-page mapping, on-page optimization priorities, and new page recommendations for medical device contract manufacturing.

1 Technical SEO Issues
10 issues
🚨
Zero Meta Descriptions Site-Wide Critical
No page on qmd.net has a meta description. Every page is missing this field — confirmed across all verified pages. Google auto-generates snippets from body text, resulting in irrelevant, keyword-poor search previews.
🚨
No Product Schema on Any Product Page Critical
15+ product pages exist with only WebPage, BreadcrumbList, WebSite, and Organization schema (Yoast defaults). No MedicalDevice or Product structured data anywhere — zero rich result eligibility for product searches.
🚨
Generic Title Tags — No Target Keywords Critical
Every page uses the pattern "[Page Name] - QMD: Silicone & Polyisoprene Elastomer Manufacturer." Target keywords are absent from titles. High-value terms like "arterial catheter," "vessel loop," and "gastrostomy button" do not appear in their respective page titles.
✅
Canonical Tags — Present (Yoast SEO) OK
Self-referencing canonical tags are correctly implemented on all pages via Yoast SEO (e.g., <link rel="canonical" href="https://qmd.net/capabilities/" />). No action needed.
⚠️
No Hreflang Tags — 6 Countries Warning
QMD operates in the US, Israel, Germany, France, Slovakia, and India — but no hreflang tags are implemented. Google cannot identify the target country/language for each version, potentially serving incorrect content to international visitors.
⚠️
Locations Page Missing H1 Warning
The /locations/ page has no H1 tag at all. This removes a primary on-page relevance signal for location-based and company-level queries.
⚠️
Thin Content on Product Pages Warning
Most product sub-pages contain 200–500 words. Clinical Areas has ~400 words; Biometrix Products has ~280 words. Minimum viable depth for competitive product pages targeting B2B medical device buyers is 800+ words.
⚠️
No FAQPage Schema Warning
No FAQPage structured data exists anywhere on the site. FAQ rich results are a strong visibility driver for B2B technical queries. Product and capabilities pages are prime candidates.
ℹ️
Homepage Title Starts with "Homepage" Info
Current title: "Homepage - QMD: Silicone & Polyisoprene Elastomer Manufacturer." The word "Homepage" occupies the highest-weight position in the title tag — replacing it with the primary keyword phrase immediately improves keyword prominence.
ℹ️
About Page H1 is Just "About" Info
The About page H1 is a single word with no descriptive value. While About pages carry lower commercial keyword weight, a descriptive H1 ("About QMD — Global Medical Device Contract Manufacturer") adds context for crawlers and users alike.

4 CDMO Keyword — Decision Note
⚠️
cdmo — 18,360 vol/mo — Not Mapped to Any Page
cdmo 18,360 searches/month (US)

Why it's unmapped: The SERP for "cdmo" is dominated by pharmaceutical CDMOs — Lonza, Samsung Biologics, Recipharm, Catalent. These are drug substance/drug product contract manufacturers, not medical device manufacturers. A standalone QMD page targeting "cdmo" would compete in a pharmaceutical SERP where QMD has no authority and no relevance signal.

Recommendation: Use "cdmo" and "medical device cdmo" as supporting terms within the Homepage and Capabilities pages — not as targets for a standalone page. Specifically: include "medical device CDMO" in the homepage title and body copy, and in the Capabilities page description. This captures navigational and branded "medical device cdmo" searches (80/mo) without building into a mismatched SERP.

Revisit if: SE Ranking shows a SERP shift toward medical device CDMOs, or if QMD wants to build authority in pharma-adjacent device manufacturing (combination products, drug-device). At that point, a dedicated /medical-device-cdmo/ page with a pharma-bridge angle would be justified.

How to read this section
  • REWRITE — replace the heading text with the keyword-bearing version. Every proposed heading matches content that is already visible on the page. REWRITE + RETAG — new text and a new heading level together.
  • RETAG — the text stays, only the heading level changes. In Elementor this is the HTML Tag dropdown on the Heading widget — the visual styling does not change.
  • ADD — a short new heading where a block of content has none. CONTENT FIRST — a keyword mapped to the page that must not go into a heading until supporting content is added.
  • DEMOTE — the text is not a real section heading (CTA lines, table labels) and should not be a heading tag at all.
  • (Vol) — average monthly Google searches for the keyword, same source as Section 2.
  • 14 product pages currently have no H1 at all — their top heading is an H2. The first row for each of those pages converts it to the H1; the recommended levels below assume that fix is applied.
