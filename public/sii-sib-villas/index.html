Welcome to Sii Sib

A private slice of Koh Phangan, all to yourself

Tucked into a peaceful corner of Koh Phangan, Sii Sib Villas is an intimate collection of just four private pool villas set across 1,500 m² of lush, tropical grounds. With only four homes on the entire property, this is Koh Phangan the way it is meant to be felt: quiet, private, and unhurried.

Each villa is thoughtfully designed over two levels and finished to a genuinely high standard. From the moment you walk in, everything is ready. The kitchen is fully equipped down to the last fork, the beds are made, and your own private pool is waiting just outside the door. Whether you are here for a romantic week, a family holiday, or a month of sun-soaked remote work, you will feel at home from day one.
